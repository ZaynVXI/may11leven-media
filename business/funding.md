# Funding and grants (research list)

Priority: low. Marketing and showing the work comes first. Look worldwide, with the Middle East and Africa ahead of Europe (European grants are hard for an African founder). Check this list in quiet moments, not instead of the work.

Eligibility depends mostly on **where the company is registered** and on whether a studio counts as a "product" or "tech" company. Many programmes favour a product with traction; our own tools (calculator, booking agent) help there.

## Africa (needs an African founder or company; check per programme)
| Programme | What | Fit for us |
|---|---|---|
| Tony Elumelu Foundation Entrepreneurship Programme | $5,000 seed plus training, all 54 countries, businesses 0–3 years, any sector; opens yearly (last window closed 1 March) | Good |
| Google for Startups Black Founders Fund (Africa, and a third cohort covering Europe) | Equity-free cash plus Google support for Black-founded startups with traction | Good once we have revenue |
| AfricaTech Award | For monetised tech startups with traction | Later, with clients |
| Africa Business Heroes | Share of $1.5M, all sectors, impact and jobs | Possible |
| Savvy Global Fellowship | $3,000, Africans 18–40, early stage, rolling | Possible |

## Middle East
| Google for Startups Accelerator: MENA | Equity-free support (no cash), AI/ML products with traction | Good for our AI agents work |

## Austria (needs an Austrian company; Zayn is based in Graz)
| aws Preseed / Seedfinancing | Large grants for innovative, scalable products, not for service agencies | Only for a real product (e.g. a booking or AI agent product) |
| FFG General Programme | R&D project funding, 50–70% of costs | Only with an R&D project |
| aws impulse (creative industries) and SFG (Styria) | Regional and creative-industry support | To check directly on aws.at and sfg.at |
