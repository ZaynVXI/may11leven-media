# 3D and mockup fidelity: techniques with settings
## Photoreal product setup (cgaxis.com render guide)
- Cycles, adaptive sampling on, noise threshold 0.01, min samples 128; simple product shots 512-1024 samples, glass/SSS scenes 2048+; denoise OIDN on CPU.
- Light paths: max 12; diffuse 4-6; glossy 4-6; transmission 8-12 for glass; volume 2-4.
- Lights: key area 50-100 W at ~45 deg; fill area 10-25 W opposite (or HDRI 0.3-0.8); accent spot/small area 5-15 W. Warm practicals 2700-3000 K, daylight 5000-6500 K.
- Camera: 85-135 mm for products (no distortion), 50 mm for natural scenes; f/1.4-2.8 for shallow DOF, f/5.6-8 for full sharpness; full-frame 36x24 sensor; set Focus Object, never a guessed distance.
- Output: EXR for grading, PNG for alpha, JPEG for web.
## Fill = half the key (uhiyama-lab lighting fundamentals)
- Fill at about 50% of key strength keeps form; larger area lights = softer shadows, so size is the shadow control, power is the brightness control.
## For our renders
- Our ata bottle used 64 samples + denoise; for the next glass render try 1024 samples, transmission bounces 12, an 85 mm lens and fill at 50% of key, and compare noise in the glass edges.
