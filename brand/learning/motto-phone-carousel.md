# Motto × Klaviyo-style product reel: the phone carousel

Reference: `brand/refs/reels/motto-klaviyo/` (an 18-second reel and its caption).

## The presentation
- **Three phones on a ring.** The centre phone faces the camera dead on, and the side phones turn away in true perspective, like a cover flow. The ring rotates one step about every 1.5 seconds, so every step brings a new screen to the centre.
- A flat light-grey stage with no props or shadows. Only the screens carry colour.
- **The screens tell one product story in order:**
  1. A loader on brand red with the mark, counting from 10% to 95%
  2. A profile card over a cropped photo (no face)
  3. A report cover (a yoga studio's "2025 Overview")
  4. A customer detail screen: $931.54 lifetime value, a donut chart of opened emails, a bar chart of email open rates
  5. A sign-up form filling itself in, with green ticks appearing one by one
  6. A marketing page ("Unlock the full potential of your data with AI") with the generative pattern
  7. A podcast player built on the same pixel pattern
- **The system is one red, one lilac, a pink gradient and a pixel or generative pattern,** plus black and white type. Every screen is either white UI or a full-bleed brand colour, which gives the reel its rhythm.
- **The UI is real and spare:** one big number per screen, plenty of white, small mono labels, red primary buttons. Everything reads at phone size.

## The caption
1. A question hook: "What happens when you simplify the complex and design with clarity in mind?"
2. The client is described, not named: "a powerhouse in customer data and automation".
3. What they did, joined with arrows: "rebuilt the UI system from the inside out → transforming cluttered visuals into a clean, intuitive framework".
4. The system, then the motion, then the user: "Every element was rethought to highlight the user → their flow, their data, their story."
5. A proof-of-value line: "it works better", not just "it looks good".
6. A one-line thesis: "This is what it looks like when product design meets brand thinking."
7. A soft call to action: "Building something big? Let's talk." and the handle.

## What we take
- **The phone carousel is our default for app concepts.** Build it in Blender (a real phone model on a turntable ring, screens as image textures, a step rotation with ease) or with CSS 3D and Playwright. First candidates: the fade. barber app, Kora Dental and the Check tool.
- **Alternate white UI screens with full-bleed brand-colour screens,** so the reel has rhythm.
- **Every screen holds one hero number or one action** (KES 2,000 deposit, 12 in queue, verdict: scam).
- **Captions:** a question hook → the problem → what we did, with arrows → why it works better → a thesis line → "Building something? Let's talk." On LinkedIn, sentence case.
