# .raw: the website (rawlab.co, recorded in brand/refs/raw/site/)

- **Hero:** "Building experiences worldwide." in huge grotesk, with two small photos set inside the headline between the words, a tiny coordinate label (46.0396° N, 14.5075° W), and one quiet button: "View our work".
- **Services as three chapters, Design / Build / Grow,** each with a sculptural grey stone 3D object, a giant one-word title, small black pill tags for the sub-services, two lines of serif text and one "Let's design / build / grow" button. One object per service, never a stock icon.
- **Work:** three tiles, each a real object photo or a device in a hand (STOW bag in hand, a glowing mark, a phone with dark UI).
- **Who we are:** a serif statement with a stone sculpture and a white interior photo drifting behind it.
- **Proof:** press logos on tilted ribbons crossing the page (Vogue, GQ, Wallpaper*): movement without noise.
- **Close:** "Let's work together" with a team photo set inside the words.
- **UI:** a floating pill navigation at the bottom (Home, Services, Work, Pricing, Store, About, Contact), a light grey #F2F2F2 page, black type, almost no colour. A public Pricing page and a Store.

## For us (steal the thinking, never the look)
- Our own site: each of the seven services gets one object of its own (from Blender), one giant word and pill tags. Ours must not be stone and must not be grey; use our palette and our own object idea.
- Photos set inside headlines are a strong, simple move for our hero and the closing line.
- A public price range page (we already planned "from" prices; Zayn decides the numbers).
- Proof ribbons only when we have real proof; never fake logos.
