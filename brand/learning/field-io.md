# FIELD.IO: "Creative Intelligence for a Living World"

Sources: field.io and the Nike Rise, IBM Quantum Compass and Google Sensing Spaces case studies. FIELD is a creative, design and innovation studio in London and Berlin. Clients include IBM, Nike, Google, Meta, Kia and Chopard.

## How they think
- **They build systems, not assets.** "Creative systems" and "environments built to anticipate, inspire, and evolve". They deliver the machine that keeps making the brand, not a fixed set of files.
- **AI in small parts, in smart ways.** Nike Rise uses AI only to sort and crop assets, extract colours and write local stories. An AI sports caster was "investigated", not shipped. The craft and the brand stay human, and AI does the chores.
- **Data plus feeling.** Google Sensing Spaces turned biometric data from bracelets into a personal watercolour and a report for each visitor. The data becomes something you'd keep.
- **Personal first.** IBM Quantum Compass: onboarding (industry, goals) → three recommended case studies → an interactive explainer → a scannable takeaway card with an archetype (Explorer, Architect, Optimiser, Navigator).
- **A motif from the subject.** For IBM Quantum, the qubit's 0 and 1 states became a gradient that rotates "like a compass needle" to guide visitors.

## How a system project is described (Nike Rise)
Problem (scale across many stores without burying content teams) → the idea (a system for making content and managing the brand) → how it works (a store "heartbeat" of live data, a "Generative Surface" display layer, a cloud console) → principles (Responsive environments, Modular by design, Seasonal flexibility, Managed globally, Live and connected, Infused with AI) → full credits.

## What we take
1. **Sell AI the honest way:** small, useful parts. Our AI automation and agents pitch is "it does the chores (sorting, replying, booking, reminding), and people stay in charge". That matches the approval steps already in our agent concepts.
2. **Every brand we design can come with a small system,** not just files. Examples: a hotel's daily menu or welcome screen that composes itself from the brand kit and today's data (weather, events, availability); a real-estate site where an onboarding question picks three listings and ends with a takeaway card.
3. **Personal takeaways:** Check could end with a saveable card. A lodge site could give each guest a "your stay" card.
4. **Name our principles** on concept case studies, as Nike Rise does (3–6 short headings with one line each).
5. **Credit everyone** who worked on a piece.

## Concept idea for our lead niche (hospitality and real estate)
**Lake House living screen:** a lobby screen and room-TV brand system for a Lake Kivu lodge. It composes the day's welcome from the brand kit plus live data (sunrise time, lake temperature, boat departures, today's menu) and has a staff console to change the season's look. AI only writes short local notes, which staff approve. Built in HTML, recorded on a screen in a Blender lobby. Run the idea test first.
