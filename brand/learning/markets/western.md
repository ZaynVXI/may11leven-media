# Western markets: problems we can solve (core target stays Africa first)

Mindset: every post, offer and pitch starts from a real problem and the gap it leaves. Problem, then who has it, then what it costs them, then our fix, then proof, then where to find them.

## 1. EU accessibility law (Germany, France, Austria, Netherlands; Swiss firms selling into the EU)
- Problem: the European Accessibility Act has applied to shops, booking and banking since June 2025. In Germany, e-commerce operators got warning letters within weeks. In France, disability groups served legal notices on major retailers. EU sites average 25% more issues per page than US sites; professional services (+44%) and manufacturing (+41%) lag most (AudioEye, July 2026, a vendor source).
- Gap: small shops and booking sites can't afford enterprise audits, and overlay plugins don't fix the problem.
- Our fix: an accessibility redesign that also looks better (our a11y tools + web stack), plus the accessibility statement.
- Find them: Shopify/WooCommerce shops, hotels, clinics, consultancies.

## 2. African diaspora food and beauty brands (UK, US, Germany, France)
- Problem: the UK African diaspora food market is about GBP 1.5bn a year and growing 5-15% a year, driven by Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana and Sudan (The Standard, May 2026). Yet African food is "still awaiting its moment" in UK supermarkets (The Grocer). Many brands look homemade next to the competition on the shelf.
- Our fix: supermarket-ready packaging and branding, e-commerce, and launch visuals.
- Bridge: it links straight back to our core markets (the products are sourced from Africa), so it builds case studies on both sides.

## 3. DTC brands needing product visuals (US, Australia, NZ, UK)
- Problem: photoshoots are slow and expensive, and stock arrives after the launch date. CGI product imagery is now widely used.
- Our fix: Blender product renders and reels before the first unit ships (the ata case study is our proof).

## 4. Small hospitality (Switzerland, Australia, NZ, Austria)
- Problem: chalets, lodges and guesthouses lose bookings and commission to OTAs, and run slow, generic sites.
- Our fix: direct-booking sites built from the place itself, accessible by law in the EU (Inzu is our proof).

## 5. Western companies entering African markets
- Problem: brands expanding via AfCFTA, Gulf or EU partners need local adaptation: MoMo and M-Pesa checkout, local tone and imagery, and packaging for local retail.
- Our fix: we know both sides. This is the most defensible gap of all.
