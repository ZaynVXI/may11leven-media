# Studio standards digest (from Zayn's references)

One page to read before every build. Sources: motto-captions, motto-ivo, motto-ivo-web, motto-testimonial, motto-portfolio, motto-phone-carousel, obys-kopetzki, studio-krista, field-io, award-trends.

## Before designing
1. Name the problem in one sentence ("X can't do Y because Z"), in the buyer's words.
2. Find 3 tensions and the one line that resolves them (Motto).
3. Pick one motif from the subject's own world: Kopetzki's stripes, Ivo's curves, Imisozi's hill contours.
4. A palette of 1 field colour + neutrals + 1 signal, every colour named, and each with a job (field, action, selected, yes/no).

## UI craft (apps and sites)
- Giant, tightly tracked titles; one serif-italic word at most.
- Dot section labels, numbered cards with a status dot, mono micro-labels.
- Every screen shows one hero number or one action, with real data and real states (loading, approved, empty, error).
- Useful features beat brochure pages: tools, glossaries, calculators, trackers, a designed 404.
- Micro-interactions explain: hover tooltips, the active step dark and the rest pale, ticks appearing, a countdown ring.
- AI does small, useful jobs, and people stay in charge (FIELD).

## Presentation
- One idea per slide or loop: a full task on a phone in a real scene, one component on the field colour, one micro-interaction, the system logic as a diagram (Ivo).
- Devices: front-on and real hardware; a three-phone ring (Klaviyo); a phone on a dark dotted stage with side light (Kopetzki); a studio display in a dark room scrolling every page.
- Stages: warm oak with moving window light (a testimonial), light grey (product), dark dotted (premium web).

## Captions
- Lead with tension → the turn → stakes in a few words → one idea per paragraph → at most one three-beat line → the service, once and late → one ask.
- Case studies run as a series: problem, decisions (→ bullets), type and colour with reasons, one true result or "concept", link.
- Credit everyone. Never fake a testimonial or a result. No repeat projects on the same platform.

## Beating "AI could make this" (minimalism-2026)
- Empty space alone now reads as cheap. Add precision (grid, measured construction), specificity (a sourced motif, a typeface with a reason) and a human trace.
- Concepts are whole worlds (line → voice → mark → system → product → merch → site → posts), never one-off frames. Flagship 01: `brand/ideas/flagship-01.md`.
- One construction idea per brand generates the mark, pattern, UI, merch and motion.

## Business
- Sell to trigger moments (funding, a second branch, moving from Instagram to a shop, the Gulf, a hotel launch).
- Named paths with prices (Mark from $800). Every project page ends with a live link and one call to action.
- Lead niche: hospitality, real estate and interiors in East Africa and the Gulf.
