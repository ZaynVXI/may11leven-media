# One base, one neutral, one accent

## What the reference reel shows
Three hero sites, each built from three named swatches:
| Site | Base | Neutral | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Health companion | Sky Blue | White | Hot Pink |
| Video editor ("Edit at the Speed of Thought") | Midnight Black | Electric Blue (glow) | Cantaloupe Orange |
| Security platform ("Enterprise Security Without Complexity") | Deep Blue | Pure White | Bubblegum Pink |
- Before the accent, each site "looks clean". The accent is what makes it stop the scroll.
- The accent is small: one figure in the hero, a badge, a chart ring, a button. Never the background.
- The base is atmospheric (a gradient, a glow, a grain field) so one hue still has depth.
- The format itself: talking head on top, swatch cards popping in one by one, the site below changing as each colour lands.

## Our rule (already close to ours, now explicit)
1. Base 60–80%, neutral 15–30%, accent under 10%. Name all three with a hex code before designing.
2. The accent goes only on what matters: the price, the main button, the one number, the live state.
3. Pick the accent as the opposite temperature of the base (cool base, warm accent; dark base, bright accent).
4. Give the base texture or light (gradient, grain, a blurred photo from the client's world) so it doesn't look flat.
5. For us: Ink base, Bone neutral, Infrared accent. Each client gets its own three.

## A post we can make from this (no face)
Screen-only version: one client concept site on screen, the three swatch cards land one by one, the site re-colours with each. Caption: base, neutral, accent. Educational and sells branding and websites at once.

## Second reel (same method, more detail)
| Site | Base | Neutral | Accent | Where the accent lands |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tennis club ("Motion. Discipline. Result.") | Midnight Navy | Cloud White | Electric Lime | One word of the headline ("Discipline."), the rating chip, the "Reserve a court" button |
| Studio ("Clarity first. Then the system.") | Jet Black | Soft White | Flame Orange | The whole hero photo is graded orange; everything else stays black and white |
| AI security ("AI deserves the same security as software.") | Paper White | Ink Black | Cornflower Blue | Two words of the headline, the main button, an ASCII-texture mountain |
- His summary: two colours build the foundation, the third makes it memorable and shows people "exactly where to click".
- New details for us:
  - Colour one word in the headline, never the whole line.
  - The accent and the main button are always the same colour, so the eye goes to the action.
  - A light site can carry its accent in a texture (ASCII or dot pattern on a photo) as well as in type.
  - A dark site can carry its accent in one graded photo.
