## Moves worth stealing (the thinking, never the surface)
1. **Phone on a real object.** Artsa: one phone standing on a grey steel shelf edge, warm-grey seamless, hard key light. One device, one surface, lots of air. (We can build this in Blender: a steel L-profile, phone, seamless.)
2. **Hands and a lap.** Microsoft Design: a phone held by real hands on cream lounge trousers, a stack of books, top-down. Human, warm, no face. Free stock or our own photo, with our screen composited.
3. **Print as a 3D still life.** A magazine open mid-air, a cover and a cloth-bound book on black, or on a mustard velvet armchair with a coffee cup. Editorial print makes a digital brand feel real. (Blender: a book, a magazine and a sofa are buildable.)
4. **Numbered archive grids.** White page, a thin centre rule, small numbered tiles (01–11) of very different media: photo, sketch, glyph construction, a clock, a fingerprint made of text, typographic hands. It shows range inside one calm system.
5. **Glyph construction.** An "a" with its points and guides on dashed orange lines; an ampersand with handles. Type craft as content.
6. **Web reels:**
   - a centred browser window on a soft blurred gradient (lilac to amber to sage) that shifts colour as the page scrolls
   - letters of the headline scattering and reassembling
   - a laptop dead-centre on white
   - pages flipping in 3D
   - a dark canvas of floating cards
7. **Diagrams that animate.** "What makes a brand culturally relevant?": a radial star of lines drawing outwards on black, with labels. One idea, one diagram.
8. **Every project has its own colour world** again: Artsa uses warm grey with a lime signal; Microsoft Design uses cream, the four-colour logo used as tiles, and pastel squares.

## Microsoft Design resources worth using
- **Secure by Design: A UX Toolkit:** security as part of UX (threat actors next to user journeys, secure defaults, warnings for outside contacts, usability-test security actions). This fits our design-plus-security edge for apps and AI agents.
- **Promptcraft:** art direction thinking (composition, light, colour, mood, deliberate choices). We use it for art direction only, not for AI images.
- **Haptics guidelines:** for app micro-interactions.
- Articles to read: More than a mark, Designing the first five minutes, When outputs are the experience.
