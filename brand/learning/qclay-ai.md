# Qclay: how they show AI projects

## What their AI posts look like
- **One light world for all AI work:** deep navy fading into a bright, grainy sky-blue glow, with frosted-glass panels on top. Paylane (fintech) uses the same blue.
- **Agents shown working, as a live task list:** verb first, then the tool's icon, then the object, with timings. "Sorting [Gmail] incoming emails & filtering spam", "Scheduling [Calendar][Zoom] upcoming meetings", "Organizing [Notion] project documentation". In Synq, named agents (Nova, Atlas, Orion) each have a status pill ("In progress"), a dotted progress meter (74%, 41%, 22%), and an expanded step list ("Processing spreadsheets & identifying patterns · 2m 24s", "Finding relevant industry reports · 3 files", "Generating presentation drafts · Analyzing…").
- **Voice input as type:** a "Listening…" pill with a dot-matrix level meter; the spoken request fades in word by word, oldest words dimmest.
- **Crops:** the panel runs off the right edge; the empty glow carries the mood.
- **Caption:** a question ("What if your AI assistant could handle the busywork…"), then the generic studio sign-off ("We are open for new projects", an arrow list of services, "Contact details in BIO or DM").

## Their site (qclay.design)
Dubai-based ("Dubai Digital Park, Dubai Silicon Oasis"). Claims "Each of our designers was the best among several thousand others" and "We're followed by 150K designers"; "Rating 5, 24 reviews". The project ticker lists landing pages, web3, Shopify, apps and branding; no AI case studies on the site itself. The contact form is good: pick services (web development, app from scratch, UX/UI, branding, maintenance…) and **a budget bracket** (≤ $5K, $10–20K, $30–40K, $50–60K, > $100K).

## What's strong
- The task-list pattern makes an agent understandable in one glance: what it's doing, with which tool, how far along.
- One coherent light world across every AI piece.
- The budget brackets on the contact form qualify leads before the first call.

## What's generic (and why we won't copy it)
- **The aesthetic is now the default "AI look":** navy-to-sky glow, glass panels, sparkles, dot meters. It's beautiful, but anyone could make it, and the founder warned us about exactly that.
- **No failure states, no human approval, no sources.** Every agent is "In progress" and nothing ever goes wrong: no "needs your OK", no "couldn't verify", no log of where an answer came from.
- **Names like Nova, Atlas, Orion** tell you nothing about the job.
- **The caption sign-off** could belong to any studio.

## What we take
1. **The task-list grammar, with sources and stops added.** For After hours: "Checked availability · from the hotel's booking system · 0.4 s", "Locked the price · rate table, quote expires 21:58", "Waiting for M-Pesa to confirm", "Sent to reception · wedding for 40 guests". A ledger, not a progress bar.
2. **Agents named by their job,** not by mythology: "Night desk", "Quote checker".
3. **Budget brackets and service choice on our own contact form** (in KES, USD and AED for our markets).
4. **Our own light world per product.** Never the stock AI glow.
