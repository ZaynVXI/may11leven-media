# 20 posts a month: every slot has to earn its place

Metricool's free plan publishes 20 posts a month, counted on the 1st. A story uses a slot just like a carousel does, and **one post sent to two networks counts twice** (Metricool help centre). The 20 is shared across all platforms. We only post when a post clears every gate below.

## The gate (in order, no skipping)
1. **Research dossier first, before any idea:** what's in the moodboards (Motto, .raw, Nuevo.Tokyo, Holographik), what has won recently (Lovie, Webby, Awwwards, Site of the Day), real evidence of the problem (news, data, forums), and how real products in that category handle it. Write it in `brand/ideas/<slug>/research.md` with sources.
2. **Problem sentence:** "X can't do Y because Z", in a business owner's words. If we can't write it, there is no post.
3. **Idea test:** a founder buyer panel of 10, used to find objections. Every post we've tested scored 0/10 for the same reason: no real clients or results yet. That's a studio problem, not a post problem. So until we have a first client, the bar is this: the post answers at least one objection the panel raised (price, what's included, a business like theirs), and the panel finds no new objection about the post itself (unclear, confusing, design for designers). Once we have client proof, the bar goes back to 3/10.
4. **Make it in Figma and Blender at full fidelity:** a frame per slide in the studio Figma file, real UI, Blender for objects, a scene from the client's world.
5. **Gates:** anti-slop (slopbeth, gesso, impeccable), preflight Pass 0–3, grid preview, and a cold reviewer for anything that sells a service.
6. **Every post sells May11Leven:** a "why it matters to your business" line, the comment-11 ask, and service rotation.

## What wins slots (from the panels)
- Something people can try (a working tool or a live demo) beats a mockup.
- Proof from our own work in use (merch, our site, our process) beats a concept with no proof.
- A price or a "from" price, once Zayn sets one.

## Split across platforms (Zayn: LinkedIn and Instagram before Facebook)
| Platform | Per month | What goes there |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 10 | 7 feed posts (carousels, reels) and 3 stories |
| LinkedIn | 7 | Professional versions: PDF documents, sentence case, insight first, 3 hashtags, "message us here" |
| Facebook | 3 | The month's three strongest Instagram posts, re-posted a few days later |

- **One platform per day, never two.** Each post goes to a single network, and the same piece goes to another network on a different day.
- Weekday rhythm: LinkedIn on Mon/Wed/Fri mornings (09:00 Vienna, 11:00 Nairobi), Instagram on Tue/Thu/Sat, Facebook on Sundays.

## Pricing (Zayn approved)

## No repeats
- A project that has already been posted on a platform does not go to that platform again. Before scheduling, check the published history in Metricool for the project name. ata (reels on 4 and 10 Nov) and Imisozi (3 Nov) are done on Instagram and Facebook.
- A different platform is fine: LinkedIn is a new audience, so the ata and Imisozi documents can run there once.

## The Motto test (every post, before scheduling)
1. Could it sit in Motto's or Holographik's portfolio without looking cheaper?
2. Does it solve a real problem, with a researched number?
3. Is it a whole world (line, system, product, presentation), or just one frame?
4. Could one AI prompt have made it? If yes, it isn't ready.
5. Would a stranger stop, save or share it?
If any answer is no, it stays in drafts.
