# Kalypso (kalyp.so): mentor

- **Who:** product designer and design engineer, Head of Design at Method (Wellington, Aotearoa). His line: "I build products people want to interact with."
- **What he ships:**
  - **72pt**, a hand-curated library of 700+ free, commercially usable typefaces from open-source repos, sold as an app with "Link in bio" posts.
  - Method's homepage and product pages.
  - UI pieces: Crate Connect, a nav UI, a Knob component, a custom watch face.
  - Talkhuman, an MCP server.
- **How he presents work:**
  - A big plain headline, then a single-column grid of work. Each item has a short italic caption and a category label.
  - Phone and app mockups shot like objects: a foldable held by robot arms on a warm grey seamless, with a soft gradient screen and spec text set on the screen ("FONT: BDO GROTESK / WEIGHTS: 7 / GLYPHS: 795").
  - Carousel posts with a grainy accent cover ("Design inspiration Websites."), then one site per slide in a rounded frame with the URL in a pill.
- **What we take:**
  - **Make a product, not only client work.** 72pt is a studio product that markets him every time someone uses it. That is the same lesson as Motto's workbooks, and why our tools series and the 11 checks kit matter.
  - **Design engineering:** components that feel good to use (knobs, nav, micro-interactions) shown as short loops.
  - **Mockups as product photography.** One device, an unexpected but simple rig, a warm seamless, grain.
  - **Curation as content:** "sites we study" carousels give people something to save. Do it in our own frame and type, never his rounded-pill template.
- **Never:** copy his rig, his grainy orange cover or his captions. Transform.

## Sites from his "Design inspiration Websites." carousel (refs in brand/refs/inspo-sites/)
- **searchsystem.co:** Julien van Havere's archive of design references (brands, books, signage, products), all-caps mono index, with a shop. Our workspace can't reach it, so browse it on the computer.
- **interfacelineage.com:** a library of interface patterns and principles, each traced back to who invented it, with demos. An AI can connect to it (members area). Good for app and UI work.
- **inspora.design:** already on our list (web, branding, product, motion, illustration).
- **interfaceingame.com:** game UI screenshots by game and genre. Good for motion, HUDs and bold UI ideas.
- **designspells.com:** already on our list (micro-interactions and delightful details).
- **72pt (app):** free commercial fonts. Our own 76-family library in brand/fonts/library follows the same open-licence rule. BDO Grotesk (shown in his post, by Uncut Type) isn't added yet: its licence couldn't be confirmed.
