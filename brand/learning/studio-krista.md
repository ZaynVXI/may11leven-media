# Studio Krista: the niche studio model

Source: studio-krista.com (project collection, homepage, the Dart Studio Toronto project).

## What they are
- "A boutique digital agency" and "one of the leading architect and interior design branding companies".
- **One niche, served worldwide:** interior designers, architects, custom home builders and landscape designers, plus a few e-commerce and corporate clients. Clients are mostly in the US, UK and Canada, with one in the Gulf (Al Darwish Group).
- **73 projects listed,** almost all "Branding & Website", some with "Marketing". The same productised package, delivered again and again for the same kind of client.

## How they present
- **The portfolio is a list:** name + deliverable tag, filterable by client type (Interior designers, Architects, Custom home builders, Landscape, E-commerce, Business & corporate).
- **A project page:** client logo → a short intro ("a bold rebrand… a cohesive and impactful brand presence") → scope of work → **named colour swatches** (Fuscous Grey, Yellow Metal, Nomad, Soapstone, Pine Cone, Bistre) → a gallery of applied identity (stationery, Instagram templates, monogram, logo suite, a magazine feature) → **a client testimonial** → "View live site" → "Work with us".
- **The tone is calm and "considered":** "Translating tactile design into digital space." "Understated modernity, engineered with clarity."
- **Every page ends with "Book a call",** linked to Calendly.

## Why it works
- **Specialising makes them the obvious choice.** An interior designer sees 70 other interior designers' sites and thinks "they get us". Each project is proof for the next client in the same niche.
- **The same package makes delivery fast and the portfolio grows quickly.**
- **The testimonial and the live-site link** do the trust work that Motto does with long case studies.

## What we take
- **Pick a lead niche inside our markets,** while still marketing all seven services. The strongest fit with Zayn's stated targets is **real estate, hospitality and interiors in East Africa and the Gulf** (developers, lodges and boutique hotels, interior studios, architects). Western interior and architecture studios are a second market.
- **Productise for that niche:** Mark + Storefront, presented as "Brand & website for developers, lodges and interior studios".
- **Our portfolio filters by client type,** and every concept we make for the niche adds proof (Inzu Lake House is already a lodge concept, and Lumen Homes is a real-estate concept).
- **Name every colour in a palette** (as we did with Imisozi's Huye ink, Nyamasheke clay and Gakenke moss). Make it standard on every project page.
- **Every project page ends with the live site (or the concept prototype link) and one call to action.**
- **Avoid their weaknesses:** no problem statements and no results. We keep Motto's thinking (problem → decisions) on top of Krista's niche focus.
