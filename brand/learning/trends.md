# Design trends, filtered through our minimalism

Zayn's rule: always know the latest trends, so we know how people are building and which styles they use, then use them in a minimal way that fits our style. Each trend below is written as one move we can make, never the whole style.

## Sweep: web, UI and branding guides
Sources: daisyUI's 18 UI trends for 2026, KOTA's brand design trends for 2026, Really Good Designs' web design trends 2026.

| Trend | What it looks like | Our minimal move | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Type-first / typographic statements | Oversized high-contrast words as the main visual | Already ours: one huge cropped line per tile, everything else small | Brand, social, type poster |
| Glassmorphism (with solid zones behind text) | Frosted translucent layers | Soft glass and aura language; always a solid zone under text for contrast | Money and health apps |
| Tactile and imperfect | Grain, noise, scuffed edges, hand-made marks | One grain pass, or one scanned hand-made mark, on a quiet layout | Brand, packaging, hospitality |
| Cinematic heroes | Full-bleed photo or video with simple large text | Real photo, graded, one line; our After hours cover is the still version | Websites, hotels, real estate |
| Dynamic motion identity | Logos and elements that animate on scroll | One signature motion per brand (the 11 bars sliding into offset) | Brand, web, reels |
| Data-dense utilitarian UI | Compact information-packed screens for experts | Terminal and ops language: mono, tables, one hero number | AI agents, dashboards, logistics |
| Hyper-local and heritage-coded | Motifs and marks rooted in a place's history | Identity drawn from the client's place (Kusi sail, Swahili door carving), one motif only | Hospitality, food, Africa-first brands |
| Biophilic | Natural colour, texture and light | Material from the client's world (coral stone, lime plaster, wood) | Hotels, wellness, real estate |
| Interactive 3D | Objects spin or react to scroll and cursor | One Blender object per page that turns on scroll, nothing else moving | Packaging, product launches |
| Mixed scroll directions | Vertical switches to sideways galleries | One horizontal gallery for a project's process, never more | Case-study pages |
| Claymorphism, chrome, Y2K, neubrutalism, scrapbook | Puffy, glossy or loud surfaces | At most one object or one detail, and only when the product is playful | Bright consumer products |
| Controlled chaos / anti-design | Broken grids and collage on a calm system | Density in one place only (an index or a wall of work) | Studio posts, portfolio |

## Sweep: calm credibility, the guided walk, marks that move
1. **Restrained, calmer money and data products.** Dribbble Select's 2026 "Best Shots of the Year" (content-hub.dribbble.com, ultimate-dribbble-select-best-shots) names muted palettes, structured layouts and clear type as the year's thread, and singles out fintech and crypto moving from hype to institutional calm (Freeport Markets by Eloqwnt, EasyPay by Nickelfox, the Paperpillar insurance app). Fits 03 apps and 01 brand identity; languages: Soft glass and aura, Swiss data. Our move: on any money screen, the thing that could go wrong (the recipient, the fee, the arrears) is the biggest element, and the brand colour appears only on the one action.
2. **The guided walk-through, one lit thing at a time.** hontran.dev's juror list of 2026 award winners (Iventions: Three.js scenes with spotlit, installation-style light and GSAP-paced reveals; Uncommon Studio: a grid that breaks at key moments, transitions like camera moves; By-Kin: the site as one continuous surface). Fits 02 web and 07 content (reels of sites); languages: Tactile object, Warm editorial. Our move: a site or reel where each scroll step lights one thing and dims the rest, like the Ikiyaga price ladder with the light moving down the list.
3. **The mark as a motion system.** Dribbble Select 2026 calls out "Another AI Thing" (Lepisov Branding), a logo built as a motion system, and Shift Robotics (IDOL) with subtle motion cues; Lebedev's Krutoyar ships the wordmark and the emblem each with its own animation (artlebedev.com/krutoyar). Fits 01 brand identity and 07 content; language: Type poster. Our move: every concept identity gets a 3 s mark animation that shows how it's built (for us: the two bars of the 11 drop into place), used as the reel ending.

## What we skip
- AI-generated imagery as a style (it breaks our no-AI-label rule).
- Trends that become the subject instead of the product. If the trend is the first thing you notice, cut it back.

## Sweep: single interactions, scroll-run demos, data drawn as the picture
1. **One interaction is the whole post.** godly.website, Recent feed: nearly every new entry is a single component state change, not a site: Payment Swipe, Crypto Transfer Security Checks, Cinema Seat Preview, Moody Status Badges, Position Slider, Pop-Up Room Book. Fits 03 apps, 02 web and 06 AI agents; languages Bright consumer and Terminal and ops. Our move: post one real state change from our own flows (the Tuma name check going check, sending, sent, stopped) as a 4 s loop with nothing around it.
2. **The demo is the real interaction, run by scroll.** onepagelove.com/hoy: the landing page recreates the Mac menu-bar flow and plays it as you scroll, on wide white space with round avatars. Fits 02 web and 03 apps; languages Swiss data and Soft glass. Our move: the Ikiyaga price ladder stays pinned while scroll changes the floor and the photo, so the page itself is the walkthrough.
3. **The product's own data is the illustration.** onepagelove.com/soma draws each person's voice pattern as a fine botanical line on ivory; onepagelove.com/solea uses pixel waveforms on near-black with two restrained accents. Fits 01 brand identity, 02 web and 07 social; languages Technical figure and Swiss data. Our move: draw the picture from a number the product already has. Today's Miwa panel is filled to the morning's stock level (18 of 40 glasses), and the fill is the only image on the page.

## Sweep: the place as the picker, one tone, broken grids
1. **The picker drawn as the place.** PXDX's Tennis Courts Reservation App (dribbble.com/shots/24603734) draws the club as a 3 x 2 floor plan with an indoor/outdoor legend; Dribbble Select 2026 (content-hub.dribbble.com/agencies/ultimate-dribbble-select-best-shots) keeps rewarding structured, calm booking and money products. Fits 03 apps and 02 web; languages: Swiss data, Warm editorial. Our move: one drawing of the real place, the user's answer as one numeral beside it (today's coach seat picker).
2. **Monochrome with one tone.** Icons8's Dribbble trend review (blog.icons8.com/articles/dribbble-ui-design-trends; undated, so treat as long-running, not new) names black-and-white and single-tone palettes as the answer to loud colour, with examples by Samuel Octavianus and Achraf Elkaami. Fits 01 brand and 02 web; languages: Swiss data, Type poster. Our move: ink, paper and one accent taken from the place (today: Tsavo red earth #A63E25), the accent only on what the user chose and the one action.
3. **Organic shapes against a strict grid.** Lovable's 2026 trend guide (lovable.dev/guides/website-design-trends-2026) lists organic shapes and anti-grid layouts; Icons8 shows broken grids (Zhenya Rynzhuk's article grid experiments). Fits 01 brand and 07 social; languages: Type poster, Bright consumer. Our move: keep the grid strict and let one soft shape break it, and that shape must be real (today the coach's rounded ends are the only curve on the page).

## Sweep: the reading as a sentence, one app for many, glass judged on craft
Galleries were not reachable today (godly.website, land-book.com, siteinspire.com, minimal.gallery, designspells.com and dribbble.com/shots/popular were all refused from the workspace), so these come from articles, Apple's award page, a studio write-up and our saved PXDX reel.
1. **The answer up front, written as a sentence.** Webflow's 2026 trend list names "the TL;DR experience": "front-loading essentials without hiding complexity", and "minimalism in copy": brands "strip messaging down to its most essential core" (webflow.com/blog/web-design-trends-2026, last updated Dec 2025). PXDX's widget wall writes its morning tile as one sentence with only the values lit (brand/refs/pxdx-widget-wall). Fits 03 apps, 02 web, 06 agents (agent replies). Languages: Soft glass and aura (data as a calm sentence), Swiss data. **Our move:** every dashboard or widget gets one sentence that answers the question the number raises ("about 4 days of power left"), and the number appears once, in its own tile.
2. **One app instead of five.** Safaricom began moving M-PESA app users into My OneApp from 3 April 2026 to cut "duplicated systems and app switching"; the M-PESA app had over 6.5M users and mySafaricom under 3M, and the target is 10M+ on one interface (techcabal.com/2026/04/07/safaricom-migrates-m-pesa-users-to-my-oneapp-in-platform-consolidation-push). In the Gulf, DubaiNow's headline reports 5.7M payments in 2025 and 70 new services added (gulfnews.com, headline only, body not read). Fits 03 apps, 05 automation, 06 agents. Languages: Terminal and ops for the back office, Warm editorial or Swiss data for the front. **Our move:** when a client has several apps or portals, design the one home first: one balance, one search, one inbox of what needs doing, and each old app becomes a section with its own colour, never a new login.
3. **Glass is judged on craft, not used as a style.** Apple's 2026 Design Awards credited Liquid Glass in two winners, Moonlitt (Interaction) and Tide Guide (Visuals and Graphics, praised for a palette that matches the sky), and the Inclusivity category rewarded Dynamic Type and high-contrast support (apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/apple-reveals-winners-of-the-2026-apple-design-awards). Polpharma Biologics' site opens on two glass lenses over a shader gradient and swaps to CSS shapes when the frame rate drops below 30 (animorio.com/en/journal/polpharma-biologics-how-its-made). Qclay opens carousels on a single glass app icon (brand/refs/qclay/16.jpg). Fits 03 apps, 01 brand (app icons), 04 3D. Language: Soft glass and aura. **Our move:** glass only where something real sits behind it (ui-details rule 9), its tint taken from the subject (sky, water, the product's material), a solid fallback for low power and reduced transparency, and contrast checked on the worst patch behind it.
