# Project ideas bank: concepts we design, build and post
Zayn's order: don't wait for clients. Come up with useful projects (apps, sites, automations, brands, AI tools) that solve real problems, build them as concepts, and market the studio with them. Beautiful and usable, every time.
Rules: write the full problem chain (may11leven-problem-first) before designing; facts only from brand/learning/markets/ with sources, never invented numbers; rotate sectors; run founder-consumer --quick before posting (founder-idea-test.md). Status: idea → chain → concept → posted.

## How each one becomes a post
- Carousel: 1 the problem hook in their words; 2 the diagram or scroll moment; 3 to 5 the fix at high fidelity on a device in its own world; 6 the outcome (only real or clearly a concept); 7 comment 11 or email.
- Reel: record the real HTML scroll (build it as code, then the preflight) and loop the hero object.
- Label concept work as a concept. Never fake clients, numbers or reviews.
