## Thursday deep dive: what moves reach now
- Reels still win reach at our size (4.9% of followers per Reel vs 2.6% carousel, accounts <10k) but images/carousels win interaction and saves; carousels get re-shown to people who skipped them (fanpagekarma.com, blog.mean.ceo).
- Front-load: 65-76% of a post's views come in days 1-3, so the first 3 days decide it; reply to every comment in that window (blog.mean.ceo).
- Instagram search behaves like Google for services: on-screen text, captions and alt text with buyer phrases ("logo design Nairobi", "hotel website Rwanda") get found.
- "Your Algorithm" topic controls: be clearly about one topic per post (branding, web design, packaging) so we're picked when people choose those interests (thekeyword.co).
- Nigeria rule: sponsored or paid-partner posts aimed at Nigerians need ARCON pre-approval (7-day lead). Our own organic posts are not sponsored content, but any paid boost or collab targeting Nigeria must check this (kolsquare.com).
- Kenya and SA are WhatsApp-first (96-97%); a "comment 11" ask should also offer a WhatsApp route when we have a business number.
## How we use it this week
- Alternate reel / carousel; keep one image post a week for interaction.
- Write the first on-screen line as the search phrase.
- Plan the 72 h after each post: replies, one Story reshare on day 2.

## Thursday deep dive: what Instagram rewards now, and one angle per market
- Ranking: watch time, likes and sends. Likes count more from followers, sends count more from non-followers, and comments are "not the most important part by a long shot" (Mosseri, Oct 2026, via zoomsphere.com). Recommendations are a growing share of reach.
- Carousels beat single photos; reels are not required. Scheduling, hashtags and hidden likes don't change reach. A visible watermark from another app does.
- Measure sends per reach (shares divided by reach) as our main number, then likes per reach. "Comment 11" stays as the lead route, but the post has to be worth sending first.
- Africa: original research is the most sendable thing we can post. Hotel owners forward a stat about their own island; the Zanzibar study (9.6 s median, 86% past Google's "poor" line, 0 of 51 mention mobile money) is one tile per fact, with the method in the caption.
- Gulf: Arabic-first content reportedly gets 35-50% more engagement than translated English, and Saudi and UAE audiences sit on TikTok and Snapchat as much as Instagram (kolsquare.com). The UAE Advertiser Permit has been mandatory since 1 Feb 2026 for paid and unpaid promotion, and Saudi creators need a Mawthooq licence. Our own organic posts are fine; any collab or paid push needs this checked first. Test one Arabic version of each research tile before any Gulf push.
- Rest of world: Instagram auto-translates captions and audio for English, Spanish, Portuguese and Hindi. Keep on-screen text short and literal so it survives translation.

## Thursday deep dive: what changed this autumn, and where each market actually is
- Instagram (metricool.com news log, updated 14 Nov): reels can no longer be linked into a series (28 Oct), so every reel must make sense alone; carousels with music show in the Reels tab again; accounts that mainly repost others' photos and carousels are no longer recommended; profiles showing AI-generated people without the label can lose reach. "Your Algorithm" topic controls now cover the main feed, so the first line of each caption should name the topic plainly (fintech, hotels, branding).
- Ranking, per Mosseri via a Vanguard round-up (allafrica.com, 6 Oct): watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. Reels for discovery, feed and stories for people who already follow.
- Africa: in Nigeria TikTok reaches 47.8M adults (+43%) against Instagram's 10.0M (−6%), DataReportal 2026 via Vanguard. Instagram is the smaller, more business-minded room; TikTok is the street. Angle: post the reel version of every concept to TikTok as well (same file, plain-English on-screen text), and keep Instagram for carousels that a founder saves and sends to a partner. In Kenya, payment trust is the hook that travels: today's Tuma practice (name check before you send) is a post founders will forward.
- Gulf: DataReportal puts Saudi social adoption at the equivalent of 111% of the population and the UAE at 109.7%; Snapchat is still strong in Saudi and YouTube is the leading streaming service there (Communicate Online, 23 Aug 2026). Local cultural detail (dialect, Ramadan, national days) beats translation. Angle: one post a month built on a Gulf occasion, with an Arabic on-screen line checked by a native speaker before it goes out.
- Rest of world: Dribbble Select 2026 says calm, credible product design is what clients now reward. Angle: show process honestly ("Practice (12)", before and after), which reads as craft to a global audience and never needs a translation.

## Thursday deep dive: what to make now that Instagram keeps a 24-hour window
- **What changed (Oct to Nov):** Edits carousel editor (8 Oct) and Edits for Mac (5 Nov, free, auto captions, 4K, no watermark); a 90-minute "Features" test with linked Reels clips (26 Oct, Brazil then some US creators, a test only); reels can't be linked into a series since 28 Oct (Metricool). Buffer, 23 Oct, quotes Mosseri that more impressions now go to posts under 24 hours old (secondhand; no official number). Sources: giovanniperilli.com/en/blog/new-instagram-features-november-2026, metricool.com/instagram-news.
- **What it means for a small studio:** the first day decides most of a post's reach, so the post has to be ready to answer comments the day it goes out (the "comment 11" replies drafted in advance), and the story that points to it should run the same day, not the next. One kit in Edits (fonts, colours, sound) keeps every reel and carousel in our look without rebuilding it.
- **Africa (Kenya, DataReportal Digital 2026, Oct 2025 data):** 23.4M internet users; TikTok ad reach 18.4M (+34.6%), Facebook 17.0M (+25%), YouTube 12.1M, LinkedIn 6.3M members, Instagram 3.95M (+25.4%), X 2.09M (flat). WhatsApp use slipped to 53.9% (Communications Authority via allafrica.com). Angle: Instagram is the small room where founders and marketers look; TikTok and Facebook are where their customers are. Post the reel of each concept to TikTok and Facebook too, and on Instagram lead with carousels a founder will save. Today's seat picker is a Kenyan problem every family has met: a good TikTok hook ("you paid for four seats, you got four coaches").
- **Gulf:** carried over from last week (Saudi and UAE adoption above 100% of the population; Snapchat and YouTube strong). Angle for December: UAE National Day (2 Dec) is too close for a new piece to be made and approved, so plan for Ramadan instead: start the brief now, one product concept built around iftar timing, Arabic line checked by a native speaker.
- **Rest of world:** with a 24-hour window, honest process posts travel best when they show a before and after in the first slide. Angle: "Practice (13)": today's before (gradient card, "auto-assigned" seats) next to the after (the coach drawn).
