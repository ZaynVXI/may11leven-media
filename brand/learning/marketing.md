## Thursday deep dive: what moves reach now
- Reels still win reach at our size (4.9% of followers per Reel vs 2.6% carousel, accounts <10k) but images/carousels win interaction and saves; carousels get re-shown to people who skipped them (fanpagekarma.com, blog.mean.ceo).
- Front-load: 65-76% of a post's views come in days 1-3, so the first 3 days decide it; reply to every comment in that window (blog.mean.ceo).
- Instagram search behaves like Google for services: on-screen text, captions and alt text with buyer phrases ("logo design Nairobi", "hotel website Rwanda") get found.
- "Your Algorithm" topic controls: be clearly about one topic per post (branding, web design, packaging) so we're picked when people choose those interests (thekeyword.co).
- Nigeria rule: sponsored or paid-partner posts aimed at Nigerians need ARCON pre-approval (7-day lead). Our own organic posts are not sponsored content, but any paid boost or collab targeting Nigeria must check this (kolsquare.com).
- Kenya and SA are WhatsApp-first (96-97%); a "comment 11" ask should also offer a WhatsApp route when we have a business number.
