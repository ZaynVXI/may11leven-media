# Obys × Kopetzki: how an award-level site is built

References: `brand/refs/reels/obys-kopetzki/` (stills, 7 video strips, caption). Kopetzki is a Vienna dispute-resolution practice. The site is by Obys (80+ awards, 24 Awwwards Sites of the Day).

## The system
- **One giant grotesk** for page titles ("Access to Justice", "How It Works", "Glossary", "Beyond Values"), set at around 10% of the viewport and tightly tracked. The wordmark is huge in the footer, edge to edge.
- **One motif:** horizontal stripes that thin out, used as the hero band, photo overlays, footer rules and the 404. A single graphic idea carries the whole brand.
- **One accent:** a clear blue for links and the active card, plus pale blue-grey (#E2E6EE) panels for page headers. Everything else is black and white.
- **Section labels as a dot + small text** ("● Digital Tools", "● The Process", "● Plain English").
- **Numbered cards** (01–06) with a status dot top-right, a title, a one-line benefit and an action row ("Try it now →").
- **Dot-matrix glyphs** for each service: arbitration, negotiation, mediation and litigation, each a small grid of dots with one highlighted.
- **Photography:** long-exposure blur (light streaks, a rock in the sea, mountains in mist). It's calm and abstract, and never a stock handshake.

## The micro-details
- **A process list where the active step is black and the others are pale grey** (01 Connect, 02 Strategize, 03 Resolve, 04 Thrive), with a small photo and a one-line description on the active row.
- **A glossary in plain English** with an A–Z rail, filter pills and search. The law practice teaches its own vocabulary.
- **"Access to Justice" tools that do real work:** an agreement builder, virtual advice and "Pay Me Now" debt collection. The site is useful, not just a brochure.
- **"Geography of Work":** a map/list toggle with big numbers (20+ party nationalities, 10+ governing laws, 4 continents).
- **A designed 404:** "Error 404" over the stripe motif with "Back to Home".
- **Drag carousels** with ← Drag → controls.

## How it's presented
- A phone leaning on a dark, dot-perforated stage, lit from one side with a floor reflection. A laptop on the same dark dotted surface.
- A Studio Display in a dark grey room, scrolling every page.
- Pages laid out as a grid of screenshots on a muted blue-grey field.
- **Caption:** who the client is (one line) → what we balanced ("bold visual identity and the clarity expected in the legal industry") → the tools (typography, a structured grid, thoughtful motion) → a design system that grows with the content → one thesis line ("A different perspective on what a legal website can look and feel like").
