# Motto × Outten & Golden: how they post a testimonial

Reference: `brand/refs/reels/motto-og/` (a 12-second reel and its caption).

## The reel
- One phone lying flat on oak, dead top-down, with soft window and leaf shadows drifting across the wood. The phone barely moves. The light does.
- The screen is just the testimonial: white text on black, the quote in three short paragraphs, then the name, title, firm and URL in grey. No logo, no UI, no animation on the screen.
- It reads like a message someone sent them. Quiet, private, believable.

## How they think (from the caption)
1. **Case studies are a series.** "We're continuing our deep dive into the Outten & Golden case study…" One project feeds many posts: the reveal, the system, the site, the product, then the testimonial.
2. **The client is a named person with a role** ("Blake Eastman, our client and project advocate"), not "a happy customer".
3. **The quote does the selling.** It speaks to the next buyer's fear: "Rest easy if you decide to work with them on your rebrand."
4. **Motto credits the client.** "Blake and the O&G team approached the process with trust, ambition, and an openness…" They share the win, which makes clients want to be posted.
5. **Gratitude, then two links:** the full case study, and the productised service ("Motto Flagship®").
6. **The service is a named product.** Flagship® is a package with a name and its own page, so buyers know what they're buying.

## What we take
- **Never fake a testimonial.** This format waits for our first real client. When we have one, we use this exact setup: a phone on a real surface, moving light, their words verbatim, their name and role, with written permission.
- **Name our packages now.** That answers the panel's "what do I get for $800?". A first proposal for Zayn:
  - **Mark:** brand identity from $800 (name check, logo suite, colour and type, a 12-page brand guide)
  - **Storefront:** a website that takes bookings or orders
  - **Night shift:** a WhatsApp assistant that answers, books and takes payment
  - Each gets its own page with what's included, how long it takes and the price.
- **Run every concept as a series** (reveal → system → product → process), not as a one-off post.
- **Credit partners and collaborators by name** (photographers, illustrators, type foundries).
