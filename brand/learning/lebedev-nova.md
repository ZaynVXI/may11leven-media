# Lebedev × Nova Pharmaceuticals, and his portfolio

## The reels (10 videos; 8 of them 20–38 s)
Measured: 38, 35, 29, 28, 26, 25, 20, 13, 10 and 8 seconds. None carry an audio track; sound is presumably added in the Instagram app.
How they're built:
1. **The client's world as B-roll behind the screens.** Microscopes, glass capsules and bubbles, a lab worker in mask and cap, gloved hands, blue-lit test tubes. The real (stock or shot) footage runs full-bleed and slightly blurred; browser windows float on top.
2. **Fast, rhythmic cuts of real pages.** A new section every half second to a second: hero, product grid, prescription list, timeline (2005 → 2013 → 2024 counting up), team, FAQs, footer. Viewers feel the size of the site without reading it.
3. **Devices placed in the world.** A tablet tilted over glass tubes on electric blue; a phone held up in front of a microscope by a gloved hand, scrolling; a 2×2 grid of pages sliding on a saturated blue field.
4. **One saturated brand colour** (an electric ultramarine) holds 10 reels together.
5. **His own site reel:** the OS-like window clusters stack and re-stack in rhythm, over 3D renders and portraits.
6. **The award as an event:** the Site of the Day poster and Developer Award poster flutter in wind and swap with a liquid wipe.

## The website (Nova)
- **Product pages, split:** left, the pack rendered on a soft blue-to-white gradient with a shadow; right, a clean column with a mono tag ("PHARMACY ONLY", "EXCLUSIVE TO METCASH"), a big name, one line of description, active ingredients in small boxed specs (3 mg · 4 mg · 1.2 mg), and two large action blocks at the bottom ("Ask about this product", "Where to buy").
- **Trust data in the interface:** a Registration table (ARTG ID, presentation, on the register since, a green "Active" dot, a link to the public record), "Consumer Medicine Information" PDF tile, numbered side nav (01 Overview … 06 Where to buy).
- **Menu as type:** a full blue screen with a stacked list of large words (Home, About, Portfolio, Patients…), legal links small at the bottom. Shot on a phone resting on a liquid-glass tube.
**Lesson:** the most premium-looking thing on these pages is real, verifiable information laid out calmly. It's the same "trust" idea as Maisa's and as our After hours guardrails.

## His portfolio site
OS-like windows; mono uppercase type; "6 Ongoing projects" counter; "Currently building" and "Industry" columns; dated article list with READ links; live local time and "Serbia, Belgrade & New York"; "Press ESC for effects (?)". Shown on a MacBook over a perforated metal plate, and on a phone lying on a ribbed aluminium suitcase next to its red lock: the contact form ("What's next? The first conversation.").

## Rules we adopt
1. **Reels run 20–40 seconds.** Carousel loops (6–8 s) are slides, not reels. Every product or service post gets a real reel.
2. **B-roll from the client's world behind the screens** (free stock: Pexels and Unsplash video; no recognisable faces from reference material). Term: classroom, uniforms, M-Pesa kiosk street. After hours: dusk rooftops, boats, a reception desk at night.
3. **Cut on rhythm, every 0.5–1 s, with real pages.** Build the pages fully first so there's enough to show.
4. **Put devices on materials that mean something** (glass tubes for pharma, a suitcase for a designer who travels). For us: a school jumper, a hotel key on brass, a wooden reception counter.
5. **Show trust data in the UI** (registrations, receipts, logs, status dots), laid out calmly.
6. **One saturated colour per project** carrying every reel.
