# Obys: Creative Web Design Practice (what we take from it)

Obys (Design Education Series) teaches a 10-week course, from brief to final motion, on one project. It's paid ($569 to $1,499), so we study the free outline, Obys's own public work and Awwwards scoring, and build the method into our process. We transform it, never copy, and stay minimal.

## Their 10 stages, in our process
1. **Award-winning thinking and brief:** know what a jury scores before starting. Awwwards weighs Design 40%, Usability 30%, Creativity 20% and Content 10%. Brief the client properly and ask for the content and tools up front (logo files, photos, copy, access).
2. **Direction, references, visual awareness:** references used on purpose and never copied. Predict the design decisions before making them. Our version: the steal list from 3+ references (design-workflow step 1).
3. **Structure and wireframes:** organise the content into a clear structure first, then build a working **grey site**: real content, real sections, no colour or images. Check that the story works in grey before any styling.
4. **Art direction, typography, AI:** every visual decision serves the concept. Type shapes perception. Build grid and colour systems, then break them on purpose in one or two places. AI is only for exploring ideas, never for final images (our no-AI-imagery rule stands).
5. **Concept and storytelling:** one concept, then the information turned into a guided story (a beginning, a turn, an end).
6. **UI exploration:** several early screens and experiments, judged through the award lens before committing.
7. **UI system and depth:** consistency, rhythm, hierarchy and visual logic, then details that lift quality (hover states, captions, micro-type, cursor, image treatment).
8. **Finalisation and review:** polish, re-run the checklist before motion, plan the launch and award submission.
9. **Motion foundations:** motion comes from the concept and rhythm, not decoration.
10. **Motion finalisation and presentation:** tune timing and transitions, join UI and motion into one experience, and present it (our website showcase reel).

## Rules we add
- The grey site comes before any visual design on web jobs.
- Score every site before showing it on Design, Usability, Creativity and Content using the Awwwards weights. Below 7 out of 10 in Design or Usability, it isn't ready.
- One system, with one or two justified exceptions (a full-bleed image breaking the grid, one oversized number).
- Minimal always: the restraint is the premium part. Their course teaches the process, our style stays ours.
