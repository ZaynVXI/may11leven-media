# Research before every product (Zayn's standing rule)

"Every product you design, research first for how it's supposed to look or feel. Research a lot of design sites and Awwwards sites. Use them every time you're building a product."

No product gets a pixel until its folder has a `RESEARCH.md` written from the steps below. Preflight Pass 0 checks for it.

## 1. Category: what the best real products in this space look like
- **Real app screens:** Mobbin's connector needs a paid plan (tested 17 Nov), so it's out unless Zayn upgrades. Use free sources instead: refero.design, Dribbble and Behance searches, the products' own websites and app-store screenshots. Save 6–10 screens worth learning from.
- **Awwwards**: search "awwwards site of the day <category>" and "awwwards <category> app". From here, Awwwards pages give the award and jury scores (design, usability, creativity, content) but not the visuals, and inspiration pages are blocked. To see the work itself, open the winning sites in the built-in browser on Zayn's computer when it's linked, or study his screenshots. Note what won and why (type, motion, colour, structure).
- **Award and gallery sites:** godly.design, land-book.com, siteinspire.com, minimal.gallery, refero.design / styles.refero.design, details.so, designspells.com, beautifului.dev, are.na cool-websites, Behance and Dribbble searches for the category.
- **Design systems that do this well:** wise.design (money), base.uber.com (logistics), Apple HIG, Fluent, Gestalt, Geist, Spectrum, Lightning.
- Some sites block our workspace (searchsystem.co, interfacelineage.com, interfaceingame.com, qclay.design, behance.net, wearemotto.com). Use web search results, Zayn's screenshots, or the built-in browser on his computer when linked.

## 2. Zayn's references (always)
- `brand/refs/`: Motto, .raw, Holographik, Qclay, Kalypso, Lebedev, Satto, Obys, Nuevo, the inspiration-site screenshots. Pick the three closest to this product.
- `brand/learning/`: the matching study notes (qclay.md, kalypso.md, raw-site.md, obys.md, design-styles.md and others).

## 3. Decide the look and feel
- **Feeling:** three words the product must make its user feel (e.g. calm, in control, proud).
- **Visual language:** pick one from the product visual languages in `design-styles.md`. It must differ from the last product we posted.
- **Colour and material:** taken from the product's own world (place, users, objects), never a default palette.
- **Type:** from `brand/fonts/library`, chosen for this product, with a reason.
- **Steal list:** one move each from at least three sources, and what we change so it becomes ours.
- **Avoid list:** what everyone in the category does that we won't.
