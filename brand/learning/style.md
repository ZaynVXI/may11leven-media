# The house taste: minimal, simple, useful (Holographik first)
## Colour
- Greyscale ramp (black, 4-5 greys) + white + one warm off-white + ONE accent with 3-4 tints; accent only for one action or one moment per frame (ANI.AI orange button/28%, DeepBook blue Buy block, Mars orange roadmap)
- Event/culture work can go two-colour only (Bottone: red + black)
- Never shouty, never more than one saturated hue
## Typography
- One neutral grotesk for everything (ANI.AI: ABC Normal); hierarchy from a 4-step size scale (~130/75/33/18), not from extra faces
- Big numerals for the key number (28%, 8/30, 398, 2k, 30.8); tiny calm body text
- Black label bars (Brand / Mission / Vision) over small text; giant two-letter monogram bottom-left (CM)
- Wordmarks spaced by a ratio (golden ratio), construction lines shown
## Motifs and ideas
- Dot, line, dashed line as the whole graphic language; data as art (point constellations with 0.00x numerals, radar rings with a trajectory, timelines with one accent dot)
- Isometric blocks assembling (DeepBook), price wheel with one highlighted value, candlesticks on a dotted grid, code scrolling behind the mark
- Thin perspective line drawings on off-white decks
- Product names as a family rolling past (ANI Lyzer / Sense / Biome / Qlinic)
## How they present it
- Brand-book pages on mid-grey as a grid; floating UI collage on black; phone held by a silhouette hand; laptop on a concrete ledge; monitors in a dark room; white product on white; dieline line-art
- Motion: slow reveals, dots connecting, letters fading in with their grid, calm scrolls; event = posters in real streets + flash photos of the night
## Try this week
- Make one minimal-mode concept for our markets (e.g. a Nairobi health or Lagos fintech app): greyscale + one place-derived accent, one grotesk, dot/line graphics, a brand-book page grid post and a calm prototype reel
