# Motto presentation study 2: Betr, Maisa, Zenarate (Awwwards)

References: `brand/refs/motto/maisa/maisa-01..06.jpg`, frame sheets `brand/refs/reels/motto-2/`.

## What they do on Instagram (and why ours isn't there yet)
1. **One mark, one field.** Maisa: a seven-point mark, white on deep green, alone and centred. No headline, no labels. The mark carries the post.
2. **The mark moves.** A Figma screen recording of the mark assembling, scattering and regrouping, shown with the real Figma UI around it (layers panel, properties). It shows the craft and the tool honestly.
3. **One serif voice + one grotesk, nothing else.**
   - Maisa: "High stakes demand the highest standards" set in a light serif, with construction points on the letters.
   - Betr: a weight specimen (Light to Black) as a post, plus giant outline letters with anchor points.
   - Big numerals with one accent colour.
4. **Real photography with one line of serif over it.** "AI you can trust." over a calm, warm, grainy photo (people from stock, hands, sofas, laptops). The type is small and quiet, never a banner.
5. **Devices as clean frames on white or grey.** Phones and tablets cropped off the edge, black bezels, real pages (hero, case studies, an event screen). Several devices overlap in a loose grid. No tilted 3D.
6. **Product UI zoomed in.** One list of three workflow names, the cursor on one; a soft gradient (blue to mint) behind a white panel. One action button in the brand green.
7. **Merch as proof the brand is real.**
   - The mark embroidered small at the back neck of a knit polo, shot so the fabric fills the frame.
   - A business card in a steel case on fabric in the brand green.
   - The laptop on a desk in warm daylight.
8. **Awwwards moment (Zenarate):** floating photo tiles around one centred line ("VOTE NOW / AWWWARDS"), an olive accent, a serif italic emphasis ("fully *integrated*"), and an FAQ page in the same calm system.
9. **Colour discipline:** each project has 1 field colour + neutrals + 1 signal (Maisa: deep green, ivory, a small bright green dot; Betr: black, white, purple; Zenarate: white, olive). Never more.

## What we change
- Fewer elements per tile. A post is ONE of: the mark, one serif line, one device set, one merch close-up, one UI zoom.
- Show our mark moving (a Figma or HyperFrames recording of the 11 bars).
- Build merch for real (Blender renders) as proof May11Leven is a brand people wear.
- Type specimens and construction drawings of our own wordmark as posts.
- Project palettes per post (see `color-per-project.md`).
