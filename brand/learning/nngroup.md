# Nielsen Norman Group (nngroup.com): what we take

NN/g is the reference for evidence-based UX. Its articles and videos are free; its courses and the $249/year membership are paid, and we don't buy them. Study one article or video per week in the Saturday UI/UX deep-dive.

## The 10 usability heuristics (Jakob Nielsen), our one-line use
1. **Visibility of system status:** show feedback right away (a booking "Confirmed" state, the agent's run log).
2. **Match the real world:** the customer's words, not ours (KES, M-Pesa, "viewing", not "lead object").
3. **User control and freedom:** undo, cancel and back are always there; "staff take over" in chat flows.
4. **Consistency and standards:** platform patterns (tab bars, sheets) and one icon set.
5. **Error prevention:** sensible defaults, and confirm costly actions (payments, deletes).
6. **Recognition over recall:** show options (time chips), don't make people remember.
7. **Flexibility and efficiency:** shortcuts for repeat users (rebook in one tap).
8. **Aesthetic and minimalist design:** every extra element competes with what matters. This is our style, justified by research.
9. **Recover from errors:** plain-language errors that say how to fix the problem.
10. **Help and documentation:** short, in context, when needed.

Use these as the checklist in design-critique on every app, site and agent dashboard (severity 0–4; fix 2 and above).

## Ideas worth keeping
- **Complex is not complicated.** "Complexity isn't a design failure; complication is." A real-estate agent dashboard can hold many controls if the domain needs them; remove friction, not features. Minimal doesn't mean empty.
- **Simplicity is relative to the screen.** What is simple on a laptop can be cramped on a phone. Judge each layout against its device (matches our phone, foldable and laptop mockup sets).
- **Usability 101:** test with real users doing realistic tasks. About 5 users per round finds most problems (Nielsen's long-standing guidance). For our concepts, run a quick hallway test before claiming "easy to use".
- **Agent harness** (new article): how the loop between model and tools shapes user control. Read it before the next AI agents post, since control and approval are our agent pitch.
