# Motto × Ivo, part 2: how they build the website and brand

References: `brand/refs/reels/motto-ivo-2/` (8 website and brand videos, a brand guidelines spread and the tote).

## What the videos show
1. **Mark construction:** the Ivo symbol drawn on a dark grid with coordinate labels, then rebuilt on light grey with point labels. The logo looks engineered.
2. **People carousel:** team portraits on brand-green cards, each with a small status pill. Real people, consistent framing.
3. **Hero:** "Intelligence by design" in a large serif, with article cards fanned below (photography, illustration, a keyboard). Editorial, not SaaS.
4. **Colour system as motion:** the Ivy green field fills the frame, then the neutral ladder (Oatmeal, Cotton, Cloud, Slate, Umber, Charcoal), then accents with hex values, then full tint ladders from -80 to +60. Every colour is named.
5. **Logo reveal:** symbol → "iv" → "ivo" on a pale mint field. The wordmark builds letter by letter.
6. **Audience selector:** a calm portrait behind glass list items that appear one by one (In-house Legal Leadership, Legal Operations, Procurement). The site sorts visitors by role.
7. **Site on an iMac in a real room** (red chairs, warm light), cycling through product pages: Security First, Review, Assistant, Intelligence, Stories.
8. **The brand in physical space:** an "ivo Talks" stage screen counting down (10:30 → 10:22, "From Redline To Revenue") in a gallery with wooden stools. A speaker portrait on the same screen. Then ASCII art and illustration sections.

## The stills
- **Brand guidelines** with a real table of contents: Strategy (vision, values, audience, voice, brand on a page), Verbal (value proposition, persona, pillars, messaging, signature phrases) and Visual (logo suite, type, colour, photography, art, iconography, ASCII art, "power curves", illustration, applications). It's about 90 pages. The section tabs are small green pills.
- **The "Intelligence by Design" spread:** a big serif headline wrapped in numbered note cards and product toasts ("Report Ready (1) New", "Changes Implemented Now"). The product UI is used as layout ornament.
- **UI palette:** 10 hues × 8 tints, each named (Cloud, Slate, Umber, Cyan, Seafoam, Highlight, Mint, Ochre, Poppy, Lavender).
- **Tote:** "GrowthSummit" in serif, with a line pattern built from the mark's curves and nodes, and a woven green label carrying the symbol. Merch grows out of the brand geometry.

## Type (resources Zayn shared)
- Ivo pairs **Antarctica** (Newglyph, Lausanne) for headlines and navigation with **Tiempos** (Klim, Wellington) for body copy. Both are paid.
- **Klim test fonts** are free to try for internal tests and client pitches, but not for anything published or commercial. So no social posts with them.
- **Newglyph** starts at about CHF 200 per family and has a **student discount**. Zayn is a student, so ask him before buying.
- Until then we keep free type that does the same job: Inter Tight (grotesk) and Instrument Serif (editorial serif).
