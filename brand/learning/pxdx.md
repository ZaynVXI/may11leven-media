# PXDX study log

## Widgets experiment: the data lives inside a sentence
Refs: brand/refs/pxdx-widget-wall/ (f00.jpg, f06.jpg and a 1 fps sheet from brand/motion-refs/reference-2-widget-wall.mp4, 12 s, 720 x 900, 30 fps; the avatar photo is pixelated on our copies). dribbble.com/PXDX, pxdx.pl, Instagram and Pinterest could not be read from the workspace today (the fetches were refused and search returned nothing new), so this is the unstudied piece in our motion refs.
- **Product idea:** not one product: a home-screen widget set where each widget shows one live number and nothing else (volume, three goal rings, weather, a dot game, a clock, a 30-day activity grid, a morning sentence, a music player). The problem it plays with: widgets usually shout one big number with no meaning around it.
- **Structure:** one 4:5 frame, a 4 x 3 grid of square tiles that bleeds off both sides (we see fragments, so it feels like a bigger wall). Two light tiles (clock face, time disc) break the dark set. Bottom left: "Widgets" and three grey lines, "Interaction design / Experimentation phase (13) / Figma Motion + Claude".
- **UI details (ui-details.md):** every tile has the same outer radius and one inner padding (rules 1, 2); idle data is grey, live data white (rule 11); one cold accent (ice blue, about #7FB3D5) used only for progress; the morning tile is a sentence where only the values are white: "Good morning, **Alex**. It's **14°** and **cloudy**. You have **3 meetings** to do until 4 pm". The icons sit on the text baseline (rule 14).
- **Motion:** every tile changes its value in place inside a 12 s loop, one after another, never all at once: rings draw from empty (about 1.5 s), 14° becomes 17° with a dim crossfade at about 3 s, the activity grid fills column by column, "cloudy" becomes "sunny" and "3 meetings" becomes "2", play turns to pause at about 1 s and back at 11 s. In the last 2 s everything drains back to empty, so the loop seam is invisible.
- **Caption and workflow:** the frame is the caption again, with the rep count (13). Same method as the player: one idea, many reps.
- **Our move:** **write the reading as a sentence and light only the values.** Words grey, numbers and names in the ink colour, and when the data changes only the lit words swap, with a 16 px rise. Ours differs: the sentence answers the user's real question (how long will my power last?) instead of greeting them, and it never repeats the number shown in the widget beside it. Used today in brand/practice/fri-mita-widgets-after.jpg and the loop practice/reels/mita-widgets-loop.mp4.
- **What would beat this?** Their sentence is a greeting with weather. Ours turns three facts the user would have to calculate (units left, usual use per day, when to top up) into one line they can act on.

## Tennis Courts Reservation App: the picker is a map of the real place
Refs: brand/refs/pxdx-tennis/01-tennis.jpg (dribbble.com/shots/24603734, tags: retro, vintage, iOS). Nothing newer than SAMUR.AI on dribbble.com/PXDX this morning (profile page read; the image came from its cdn.dribbble.com thumbnail). Faces in the photo and avatars are pixelated on our copy.
- **Product idea:** booking a court at one club (Chestnut Avenue). The problem: a club's courts are not equal (indoor, outdoor, premium), and a plain list hides that. The app shows the club as you'd walk it.
- **Structure:** two phones on a full-bleed photo of an indoor court. Left: a vintage wooden racket as the hero photo with a stacked retro wordmark ("TENNIS / COURTS / RESERVATION"), court pills (Chestnut Av. B2, B1, ...) riding the photo's bottom edge, then a white sheet: date tiles, time slots, price and "Book now". Right: the court screen: a photo of the court with "Top 10" pill, then a dark sheet with "Choose a court" and a 3 x 2 grid that is the club's floor plan (A1 tall and empty, B1, B2 selected, C1 premium, B3), a coach upsell card, a full-width "Make a reservation" and the rule text. A tan band under the phone: "Get subscription pack".
- **UI details (ui-details.md):** the court grid uses the place's own logic: a legend "A,C Indoor / B Outdoor" instead of icons (rule 6 in spirit, labels where the decision is); B2 is selected with a lime outline and a check dot in the corner (rule 5, dot carries a ring); C1 premium has its badge as a small pill inside the tile (rule 6); the date row only shows days with slots (13 Mon, 14 Tue, 17 Fri, 19 Sun), so a full day never appears as a disabled tile; the unavailable time slot is quieter, not hidden (rule 11); the money rule sits under the price: "a deposit of 50% ... will be collected. The remainder ... after the training session". One accent (lime) on dark, one on white.
- **Colour:** taken from the photos: clay tan, racket-varnish yellow, net green. The lime is the ball.
- **Motion:** still shot, none shown. Implied: the court pills scroll sideways over the hero photo.
- **Caption and workflow:** no caption text reached us; no workflow shown.
- **Our move:** when the choice is about a place (a seat, a room, a court, a desk, a plot), the picker is a drawing of that place with the real legend (indoor/outdoor, window/aisle, sea view), never a list. Used today in brand/practice/thu-coach-after.jpg: a train coach drawn as the seat picker, with four seats in one block.
- **What would beat this?** PXDX shows which court is free, but not what you came for. Our map answers the user's real question in one numeral ("4 seats, together") and puts the rule that costs money (cancel up to 48 h before, 30% fee) beside the total, like their deposit line, but on the same line as the price.

## NOAL soft-drink bar: the required notice told in the product's voice
Refs: brand/refs/pxdx-noal/01-noal.jpg (dribbble.com/shots/25039049, "Soft Drink Bar Website"). Nothing newer than SAMUR.AI on dribbble.com/PXDX this morning (shot pages now answer our workspace with a 202 challenge, which we don't get round; the image came from the profile page's thumbnail on cdn.dribbble.com). Picked because it's a website and today is web day.
- **Product idea:** NOAL, a no-alcohol drink bar with a drive-thru, for "car guys". The problem: a bar for drivers has to be dry, so the brand turns the limit into the whole concept. Every name is car talk (Racing Fuel, V8 Drink, Healthy Nitro, Red Light Negroni 0%).
- **Structure:** one bento frame over a full-bleed photo of a classic car's tail light. Four tiles above (a round-cropped glass on racing green, a red "Drinks of the day" card with date and a ml list, a big negroni photo card with "Only $9.99" and Next, the logo panel cut into the photo), one dark strip below (square logo, "Our top 0% drinks for car guys", three round product shots with one in green).
- **UI details (ui-details.md):** the outer frame wraps the tiles with one big concave curve that hugs the logo panel (nested radius, rule 1); the age gate is a small glass toast inside the hero, with a round "16+" badge and a pill button, label inside the control (rule 6); drink rows carry an icon, name and a ml quantity, idle and equal (rule 11); the best seller in the lower strip is marked by a red crown dot with a ring (rule 5) and the selected drink by a raised green tile (rule 3); one icon set, thin stroke (rule 12); "Chill, I'm 16+" is the one line of personality, on the least serious action (rule 13).
- **Colour:** sampled from the scene. Tail-light red becomes the menu card, the car's paint gives the grey frame, racing green the two accent tiles. The UI looks like it belongs to the photo.
- **Motion:** a still shot, so none shown. Implied: the negroni card is a carousel (three dots, Next), the toast would slide in once and leave.
- **Caption and workflow:** no caption text reached us. Not shown how they train Claude on this one.
- **Our move:** the notice the law makes you show (age, cookies, allergens, delivery area) becomes a small toast inside the hero, in the product's own voice, with the way out as easy as the way in. It never covers a control. Used today in brand/practice/wed-miwa-after.jpg: "One cookie keeps your order. Can we count visits too?" with "No thanks" and "Yes, count" the same size, sitting as a band at the top of the drink panel.
- **What would beat this?** NOAL's gate is a joke button (anyone taps "I'm 16+"). Ours carries a real choice the user can refuse, and refusing costs nothing: the order still works.

## Player experiment and podcast UI details: one component, numbered variants
Refs: brand/refs/pxdx-player/ (frames from brand/motion-refs/reference-3-player.mp4, 12 s, 720x900) and brand/refs/pxdx-dribbble/podcast-ui-details.jpg (Dribbble "UI details of podcast app"; faces in the thumbnails pixelated on our copy). Nothing new on dribbble.com/PXDX since our last sweep (newest is still SAMUR.AI), so today is a deep read of two saved pieces.
- **Product idea:** the player is not a product, it's a training rep: one control (play/pause) explored until it feels right. The podcast piece is a calm desktop podcast app whose problem is clutter: the sidebar collapses to an icon rail and keeps the same state (badge "3", downloading 77%, a profile with a notification dot).
- **Structure:** the player is one 4:5 frame: a light stadium shape on black, the hero control in the middle, the title "Player", three grey lines under it ("Interaction design / Experimentation phase (57) / Figma Motion + Claude"), then four small numbered variants, 01 to 04. Each variant changes ONE property: 01 adds a track label, 02 a ring around the button, 03 a waveform under it, 04 a tick dial. The podcast shot shows the expanded and collapsed sidebar side by side over one scene (a concrete room with a desert behind glass and glowing spheres).
- **UI details (checked against ui-details.md):** nested radii everywhere (player tile, orb, track; the selected nav row's icon sits in its own circle, rule 1); status tone-on-tone (red badge only for the count that matters, grey badge for "2", rule 4); the Dark/Light segmented control uses the raised-tile selection (rule 3); the collapsed rail keeps the notification dot with a ring (rule 5); label inside control ("AirPods" next to its own glyph, rule 6); idle rows grey, active row white (rule 11).
- **Motion:** play turns to pause through a dim cross-dissolve of about 0.5 s (the icon fades down to grey, the new glyph fades up), never a flip. The orb glow breathes; the side ticks pulse like a level meter while playing. Nothing else moves. 12 s loop.
- **Caption and workflow:** the frame itself is the caption: "Experimentation phase (57)" says this is the 57th rep and "Figma Motion + Claude" names the tools. They show the count openly, which turns practice into content.
- **How they train Claude:** reps, not one big prompt. One component, many numbered variations, each one a single change, kept as a numbered set.
- **Our move:** show a component as a hero state plus a numbered strip of real states, each tile changing one thing, and put our practice count in the caption ("Practice (12)"). Ours differs: the strip is the component's real states in a real flow (check, sending, sent, stopped), not decorative variations, and the frame carries the product's problem in one line. Used today in brand/practice/thu-tuma-after.jpg.
- **What would beat this?** Their variants are pretty but say nothing about the user. A strip where each tile is a moment in someone's day (the Hakikisha-style name check: check, sending, sent, stopped) explains the product in one glance, and in motion each tile would play its own 2 s state loop.

## "Just UI details" with one message (peace post, 5+ slides)
Refs: brand/refs/pxdx-peace-details/. Every component in the post carries the same idea (peace): interaction cards ("How was your sleep?" then "Is it because of bad news?"), a text container shown mid-edit with a Figma selection box, a folder list, a wallet module, a confirmation dialog where "yes" is disabled and the cursor gets a no-entry badge, a content card. Caption: "Just UI details. We wish everything could be this simple…" then the themed sign-off.
**Details worth taking:** one brand gradient (indigo to rust) reused on every accent (pill, folder, wallet, card top); cards sit inside a faint outer halo frame (a second, larger rounded shape at low opacity: nested radius again); photo tiles tilted a few degrees, stacked, with thick white borders; black pill buttons with a long soft shadow; the disabled state told as a story (cursor + badge); a tiny centred caption naming each component; dark silk and light grey backgrounds alternate slide to slide.
**Our move:** a details post can carry one human message from our markets (for example "rest" for hospitality, "pay on time" for fintech), told through real states of our own components. One gradient per post taken from the product's world.

## UI Color Set series (8 posts)
Refs: brand/refs/pxdx-color-sets/. One fixed template, a new palette each post: a hero photo card with a serif title ("UI Color Set") and a glass name pill (Texas Sunset, Candy Golf, Night Ride), a "Colors" pill, a tilted swatch card with a circle photo, four dots and a Copy button, a small tilted photo tile with a check badge, a tall rounded bar and a big circle in the palette's two accents, and a 5 x 3 hex grid at the bottom (each column a colour with two lighter tints). Light posts and dark posts alternate.
**Why it works:** useful (people save palettes), repeatable, every post looks different but the frame never changes.
**Our move (needs Zayn's OK as a series):** "Colour from a place": one palette per post taken from a real African or Gulf place or material (Lamu limestone, Kigali red earth, Zanzibar indigo cloth), shown on our own components, hex grid with tints, free stock photos only, no faces in the hero.

## The bar for the next project (Zayn, 19 Nov: "our next project should be better than these")
After hours and Term v2 set the floor, not the target. Gaps against PXDX that the next product has to close:
1. **One signature move per product.** PXDX products each have a thing you remember (a 3D glass key, an ASCII face, a colour set). Ours had good screens but no single move. Pick it in the brief, from the 3D UI, ASCII or transitions skills or something new.
2. **Motion with physics, not fades.** Our loops ease in and out. PXDX uses springs, overshoot and stagger, and things touch each other. Use spring curves and let one element push another.
3. **Fewer flat text slides.** Our manifesto and offer slides are type on a dark field. PXDX makes even the text slides visual (UI cropped huge, a detail magnified). At most one pure-text slide per carousel.
4. **A hero nobody has used.** Check `hero-ledger.md`. Build the set, props and light for this product only.
5. **Colour from a place, chosen first.** Write the product's colour set (4–6 swatches with names) before any screen, PXDX colour-set style.
6. **Sound.** PXDX reels carry designed UI sound. Add soft clicks, ticks and a landing sound to the reel cut.
7. **Daily check.** Each morning's PXDX study ends with one line in this file: "What would beat this?" The next project's brief quotes the best three.
