# Shoot your shot

## What the reel says (from its captions)
- He made one Instagram post breaking down what he would do if he ran his dream client's marketing, after days of research. It got a few thousand views. The client commented, booked a call and hired him for over a year.
- Others do the same: a video on what they'd do if they ran a known brand, and the subject comments.
- Three rules: share ideas without asking permission; share them with the whole world, not only the person you want; put them in a shareable format.
- Visual style: warm paper background, dot halftone, one coral accent, simple circles and squares as diagrams, cut to the talking head.

## How it fits our rules
- A public concept about a real business is ours to publish, but it is about them, so: only with Zayn's approval, generous and true, no private data, no mocking their current work, clearly labelled "unsolicited concept".
- Tagging or messaging the business counts as contacting them. Zayn decides that, every time.
- It replaces cold outreach: the work is the pitch, and the business comes to us.
