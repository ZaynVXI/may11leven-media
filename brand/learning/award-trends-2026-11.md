# Award-winning work and web trends: what we take (Nov study)

References: `brand/refs/reels/wdt-2026-11/` (web.design.trends grid screenshots, the Axon Labs fintech concept stills and frame sheets a–e). Sources: lovieawards.com, winners.lovieawards.com, winners.webbyawards.com.

## 1. Award winners solve a problem first
The Lovie and Webby winners that stand out are not the prettiest sites. They fix something specific, and the craft makes the fix feel effortless.
- **Daisy vs Scammers** (VCCP for O2, Lovie Gold): an AI "grandmother" that keeps phone scammers talking so they can't reach real people. A real harm, a simple idea, a character people remember.
- **Signs** (DEPT®, Webby, Accessible Technology): teaches sign language with the camera giving feedback.
- **Corti Assistant** (Framna, Lovie Gold, Health): an assistant that takes notes in clinical consultations, so doctors look at the patient instead of the screen.
- **WWF Blue Corridors** (Ode Partners, Webby, Data Visualization): makes whale migration routes visible so people can see why they need protecting.
- **Opera Neon** (Lovie, Best AI Agent): an agent browser that does tasks for you.

**Rule:** every concept we post starts with a sentence of the form "X can't do Y because Z". If we can't write that sentence, it's decoration.

## 2. What the web.design.trends grid shows (the 2026 look)
1. **The device is placed in a scene from the client's world.** A laptop on a stone block in a concrete room, a monitor on a brushed-steel plinth, a laptop in autumn leaves for a delivery company, and a laptop hung from climbing ropes on grey (Axon). The prop tells you the industry before you read anything.
2. **One giant type moment per hero**, either huge and cropped ("SANTAL", "0859") or a single serif line ("You Believed", "Scroll into sanctuary.").
3. **Scroll-driven product stories.** "Scroll to fit the kitchen", "Scroll to build your backyard": the page builds the product as you scroll. The interaction *is* the pitch.
4. **One accent colour on near-neutrals.** Axon uses grey, ink and one orange. Lumio uses green and white. Nothing is more than three tones.
5. **Real numbers in the UI.** 118 g protein, 2,300 kWh, "12 unprofitable accounts found in week one", "18% margin recovered". The proof sits inside the design.

## 3. Axon Labs concept, frame by frame (the bar for our concept work)
- **Problem in the headline:** "Make complex data human readable." / "Axon starts where spreadsheets stop."
- **Hero object:** one 3D hardware-like "connector" in orange and white. It's a metaphor you can hold.
- **Process as a numbered rail:** 01 Unify, 02 Map, 03 Model, 04 Explore, 05 Trace. Each step has its own isometric illustration: CRM, Banking, Payroll, Billing and ERP boxes joined by orange lines, with "insight" pins that appear.
- **Ingest → Attribute → Connect cards:** a dark card, then two light cards. The motion is small (bars slide, nodes link up) and every animation explains a step.
- **Proof block:** a quote plus two big numbers (12 / 18%) and "Read the full story".
- **Closing CTA:** "Trace your first number" with the orange object rising from below. The CTA is a task, not "Contact us".
- **Presentation:** a laptop on dark glass, scrolled slowly; stills of the laptop on ropes; full pages on a dark grid.

## What we change (applied from today)
- **Concepts come from problems in our markets** (East Africa, the Gulf), labelled "concept", with honest numbers (targets or test results, never invented client results).
- **Each concept gets:** a problem headline, a hero metaphor object rendered in Blender, a numbered process rail with isometric steps, one proof block (a real test, prototype metric or panel finding, labelled as such), and a task CTA.
- **Present it in a scene from the client's world** (a site on a laptop on a market crate, a clinic app on a steel counter) with a slow scroll recording.
- **Every service post earns a "why it matters to your business" line** (the type study panel's biggest objection was "design for designers").
