# Packaging and merch: what's current
## Packaging - the opportunity
- Nigeria: many locally made OTC and FMCG products have bland colours and weak descriptions next to better-designed Asian imports; exports reportedly rejected abroad for haphazard packaging; firms slow on flexible, digital-print and sensory packaging; young middle-class buyers drift to foreign brands (businessday.ng, older article - re-check)
- Ghana/Uganda: packaging and labelling gaps block SME exports; SMEs lack awareness of standards (graphic.com.gh 2014, businesstimesug) - the gap is long-standing
- China: guochao ("national wave") - Gen Z buys local brands that mix heritage with modern design; plain minimal packs can feel cold; cultural references must be subtle and historically right (Tang patterns, weaving), never stereotypes; limited festival editions (Mid-Autumn) work; foreign brands must co-create with Chinese partners and avoid geopolitical missteps (kas.de)
- India: D2C brands compete on unboxing on a budget, minimal "less is more" packs, sustainable materials (echai.ventures, pitchonnet)
## Merch - what's current
- Merch is shifting to tactile, limited, story-led objects over logo dumps: little treats, collectables and blind boxes, region-specific runs, multi-sensory and textured items, personalisation, reusable and locally made, nostalgic/retro, calm daily-ritual objects (insights.fluidbranding.com)
- Studios Zayn sent: Obrazur and Blacklead put identities on industry objects (ratchet straps, caps, totes, jackets, lighters, mugs) cut on the beat; WOB shoots merch in styled scenes
## How we use it
- "Pack rescue" series: a fictional or permission-given local product (shea butter, spice blend, juice, pharmacy OTC, tea) - honest before (generic) vs our after; label concept; never mock a real business.
- "Shelf test": our pack next to imports in a market-stall scene - readable at 3 m, one hero colour, local language plus English/French/Arabic/Chinese where it sells.
- Export-ready packs: barcode, nutrition, ingredients, origin, certifications laid out beautifully (micrographics) - sell it as "ready for the shelf abroad".
- China: only with cultural care - modern + heritage, Chinese typography done right (research a free Chinese font like Noto Serif/Sans SC), festival editions; platforms there are WeChat, Xiaohongshu (RED), Douyin - note for strategy, we post on IG/FB.
- Merch concepts for our own brand (11 mark on objects: lighter, enamel pin, cap, tote, notebook, coffee cup, keyring, tape measure "11 days") and for concept brands in their industry's objects.
