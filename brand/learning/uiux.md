# UI/UX: what's current
- Tools: UI/UX Pro Max (reference library), UI Skills baseline-ui (anti-slop), prototype recorder, GSAP/Motion/Lottie/Rive.
- Anti-slop: no purple gradients or glow, one accent per view, balanced headings, tabular numbers.
- Holographik minimal UI (ANI.AI): white screens, huge air, tiny calm text, one accent colour for one action or one moment, big numerals for the number that matters (28%, 8/30, 398), dot-and-line charts, AI chat 'Ask anything' bar; spacing specs drawn as content (16px/12px annotations on line-art devices) (behance.net/Holographik)

## Sat deep dive: availability pickers (booking a scarce slot)
The pattern: when the thing is scarce (gorilla permits, a lake room on Eid, a clinic slot), the picker is the product. Rules from today: list the days, don't make people hunt through a month grid, when the window is short; put the count left on every row; grey out only what can't be booked; keep the selected row large and inverted; anchor the price and the one action to the bottom (NN/g date input; Holographik's wheel).
- Africa: Volcanoes NP permits are USD 1,500, up to 8 trekkers per family, about one hour with the gorillas, ages 15+, reporting around 07:00 at Kinigi. Booking runs through RDB on Irembo or an operator, with full payment upfront. One source says 112 permits a day (up from 96 in 2024), but the same page also says 96 and gives both 12 and 14 families, so we show counts left per day, never a "total" we can't stand behind (dumaexplorer.com). The low-season bundle (USD 1,050 with Akagera and Nyungwe, November to May) belongs right under the price, because that's where the decision happens.
- Gulf: build RTL from day one. Mirror arrows, chevrons, sliders and swipe direction, but never logos or photos. Plan for Arabic running 25 to 30% longer (no fixed-width buttons). Use Arabic UI faces such as IBM Plex Arabic, Cairo or Tajawal. Show Hijri next to Gregorian dates in booking and real-estate flows, since peaks follow Ramadan and Eid. Use AED pricing and regional gateways (PayTabs, Telr, Network International), with UAE PASS for identity where the app touches government (tekrevol.com). A picker for a Dubai desert camp: an Arabic day name, the Hijri date small under the Gregorian one, and Friday and Saturday marked.
- Rest of world: the expectation is set by Airbnb and airline calendars (two months side by side, the range drawn as one shape, the price on each day). For a premium safari brand the list beats the grid: fewer, larger rows read as considered and work one-handed.
