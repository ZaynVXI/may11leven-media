# Motto workbook bundle reel (23 s, 9:16)

## What happens
1. **0–2 s:** a tiny flag icon alone on off-white. Nothing else on screen.
2. **2–5 s:** the headline builds word by word, spread across the frame, then gathers into one centred block: "The essential brand building workbooks". The last word is grey, then turns black.
3. **5–17 s:** one slow vertical scroll through a list of six grey tiles. Each tile has:
   - a small arrow label (→ BRAND ARCHETYPE WORKBOOK);
   - one workbook cover in the middle;
   - a number in brackets (04) and a short line on the right ("Find your character").

   The lines are imperative, five words at most: "Start with questions, not answers", "Know why you exist", "Define your core values", "Choose a strong name".
4. **One accent:** five covers are cream line art. One is bright green, and that cover stops the eye halfway through the scroll.
5. **17–20 s:** "Six workbooks. One brand." with one line under it.
6. **20–23 s:** a black offer card slides up: Black Friday, 20% off, a code inside a pill, and the shop link. The offer is time-limited and real.

## What to learn
- **A studio can sell a product, not just hours.** Motto turns its method into workbooks people buy and use. Every buyer meets the method before they ever hire the studio.
- **The method gets names.** Six steps, each with a short imperative line, so the list reads as a framework.
- **One accent in a neutral system.** Grey tiles and cream covers, with one green cover. The same rule as our one-Infrared rule.
- **Motion is a scroll, not effects.** Type builds, one steady scroll, then one card. No transitions or stickers.
- **The offer comes last and is honest.** It is a dated seasonal reason, with the code shown in full.

## Our version (transform, never copy)
- **Product:** a free "11 checks" kit, one page per check, built on our document system (`brand/templates/documents`):
  - brand at a glance;
  - homepage on a phone;
  - booking path;
  - WhatsApp reply time;
  - photos;
  - and so on.

  It is free, so it creates leads and proves the method. A paid deepening can come later, Zayn's call.
- **Reel:** our 11 mark alone → the title builds in Inter Tight → one scroll through numbered check tiles ("(03) Count the taps to book") with one Infrared tile → "Eleven checks. One afternoon." → an end card: "Comment 11 for the kit" or the link.
- **Offers:** only real ones. Black Friday is close; a free kit fits our no-discount, premium stance better than a price cut.
