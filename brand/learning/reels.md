# Reel study notes
Daily: 3 sheets from brand/refs/reels/ + 1 external source. Rules: hook in 1 s, one idea per reel, sound to the cuts, loop or end on the mark.

## Study: merch flash loop, a talking-head list reel, a laptop-on-stone site reel
Sheets: reel-3113c18e (merch, hands only), reel-24210ed3 (creator "5 plugins" list), reel-1a6bfe20 (Holographik CoMinVi laptop on rock).
- Hook (first second): merch reel opens mid-action on the lit lighter, no intro. Creator reel opens on a full-sentence caption at the top ("Don't use X without these 5…") = question + number. CoMinVi opens on a black screen with a tiny mark, i.e. a slow hook that only works because the laptop-on-rock still is already striking.
- Cut rhythm: merch = strict A/B alternation (lighter / beanie) every frame of the sheet, ~0.5 s per shot, 8 pairs, perfect loop. Creator = talking head every other shot, B-roll screen inserts ~1 s each, numbered beats 1-5. CoMinVi = one locked shot ~16 s, the screen content changes (hero, story, rock video, values list).
- Camera: merch and CoMinVi locked off; all movement is inside the frame. Creator: handheld selfie.
- Type: creator uses one-word caps captions centred low ("THESE", "PLUGIN", "NO.2") changing every word, plus a 2-line title at top; CoMinVi type lives only inside the screen (Honesty / Respect / Integrity / Loyalty, active word black, rest ~20% grey, stepping down one per beat).
- Transitions: hard cuts only in all three. No wipes, no zooms.
- Sound (from the pace): merch is beat-cut, one hit per shot; creator is voice-led; CoMinVi reads as a slow ambient bed with soft clicks on screen changes.
- Loop/ending: merch loops seamlessly (last frame = first). Creator ends on "Follow and comment 'Plugin' and I'll DM you" held 2 shots. CoMinVi ends on the values list, which loops back to black.
- Cover: merch = lighter frame; CoMinVi = laptop on rock (works as a still at 3:4).
- For a minimal feed: take the comment-keyword ending (we already use "comment 11"), the numbered beats and the opacity list. Skip the talking head, emoji and word-by-word caps captions.
## External: School of Motion kinetic type part 1 + sound-to-motion rules (cursa.app)
- Markers on the comp at every audio beat; nudge visuals 2-3 frames EARLY so the eye lands with the sound.
- Stagger elements 1 frame apart; a reveal of ~4 frames; exits ~8 frames with a long eased tail.
- One-node camera on nulls, moves overlapped; start the slow zoom-out a few frames before the main move ends.
- Sound: whoosh starts just before a transition, hit on the new state; soft tick on small highlights; only 3-5 accent sounds per piece; one music bed; test at low volume; short tails.
## Practice: "opacity list" (brand/practice/reels/opacity-list-v1/v2.mp4, 8 s, 1080x1920)
- Six services on Bone; active item Ink 100%, others 18%; Infrared dot follows the active row; big counter 01/06; ends on the 11 mark + "comment 11"; last 0.45 s returns to frame 1 for the loop. Ticks lead picture by 2 frames, whoosh into the exit, low hit on the mark.

## Reel length: ours vs the references Zayn sent
- His 92 reference reels: median 14.6 s, average 18 s. 28 are under 10 s, 31 are 10–20 s, 21 are 20–30 s, 10 are 30–60 s, 2 are over a minute (60 s and 100 s).
- Ours so far: 5–22 s, mostly 12–17 s. After hours is 22 s.
- Verdict: our typical length matches the references, but we never make the long tier (30–60 s) that a third of theirs use for case studies and process stories.
- Rule from now on, length set by job: loops and logo marks 6–10 s; product demos 15–25 s; case studies and process stories 30–60 s (one a fortnight). Length must be earned: hold each line for its reading time, and cut any second where nothing changes.
## Study: a held still with type in the corners, a poster that rebuilds itself
Sheets: reel-0148ef71 (Holographik "Colonize Mars"), reel-0986ee33 (Bottone x Holographik poster, Berlin to Zagreb).
- Hook: Mars opens on the finished frame, a planet edge lit from the right, with the title already there. No build-up; the still is the hook. Poster opens on a red poster in a real concrete underpass, so it reads as a photo before it reads as motion.
- Cut rhythm: Mars is one shot; the only change is the title stepping between corners (top-right, centre-left, bottom-right) about every second. Poster is one locked shot of 16 states where the black shape melts and rebuilds behind the type, about 0.5 s each.
- Camera: both locked. All motion happens inside the frame.
- Type in and out: hard swaps, no fades. Same grotesk caps, same size, only position changes. On the poster the type gets eaten by the shape and comes back.
- Transitions: none; the frame changes state.
- Sound (from the pace): a steady ambient bed with a soft tick on each type move.
- Cover: any frame works because the layout never changes. That is the reason to build reels this way for a minimal feed.
## External: SocialPilot reel guide (Sept 2026) + Mosseri notes (zoomsphere.com)
- Something must move within 0.5 s, and the first 3 s decide completion. A 90 s reel that's watched to the end beats a 3 min reel that isn't.
- Put a keyword line on screen and in the caption; Instagram reads both for search and for "Your Algorithm" topics.
- Trial reels (1,000+ followers) go to non-followers first. Test different versions, never the same one twice.
- No other app's watermark on export. Sends per reach is the number to watch.
- For us: a 6 s "9.6 s" reel where a phone-sized frame stays empty while a counter runs to 9.6, then the hotel photo lands. Movement starts at frame 1 (the counter), the empty wait is the story, and the cover is the final frame.

## External: visual hooks + the thumbnail test (creatorflow.so)
- Five visual hooks, each resolved by the eye in the first second: (1) motion at frame one (action already underway, must relate to the topic); (2) an object out of place; (3) the finished result first (good for tutorials, shot close and lit so it reads small); (4) a face mid-action; (5) the before state at its worst, fixed fast.
- The thumbnail test: screenshot frame one, shrink to ~1/3 phone width, look for one second with sound off, then write one sentence of what it promises. If you can't, change the frame. Checks: real image not a fade-from-black; one subject fills the frame and contrasts the ground; subject/text sit in the centre that survives the 3:4 grid crop (~240px off top and bottom of a 1080x1920 reel).
- For us: the Mita concept reel is hook type 3 + 5 combined - open on the finished home screen with "38.6 kWh" already huge (result first), then cut to the confusing paper token it replaces (the before). Counter and split animate in; cover is the final clean screen. Keep the numeral and split inside y 220-1500 so the grid crop and IG chrome don't eat them.

## Study: website showcase reels (Lebedev Haven, Satto services)
Sheets: reel-207abe5b (Lebedev: Haven Foods, Contour, a studio site), satto-reel-6b4df76b (Satto: services list over a film still).
- Hook: Lebedev opens on the finished collage, page screens laid over an aerial coast photo from the same project; Satto opens on a black index with the services already set in a huge thin serif. Both show the finished thing at frame one.
- Cut rhythm: Lebedev cuts every ~1 s between four plates (collage, phone on a sage cushion, tablet on a chrome stand, a dark site on a night sea) and repeats them, so the reel loops. Satto is one move: the service words stay put while the image under them changes (black, white, portrait, a figure in fog).
- Camera: locked. Devices sit still on real surfaces (fabric, chrome); any movement is the screen content.
- Type in and out: Lebedev uses no added type, the site's own type is the type. Satto's words overlap the photos and get partly eaten by them, then the last frame names the project ("Onda").
- Transitions: hard cuts only.
- Sound (from the pace): a steady beat with a cut on every bar.
- Cover: Lebedev's collage frame works as a still post; Satto's word stack works at thumbnail size because it is only type.
- External (june-mango-design.squarespace.com): the usual method records a scroll and masks it into a laptop mockup in Photoshop. We do the same in code: a Playwright 1440x900 scroll recording placed into a Blender device on a real surface, which avoids template mockups.
- For us: an Ikiyaga reel, 8 s. Frame 1 is the ladder with every price already there; the selected row steps down one flat per beat (hard 1-frame changes) while the detail strip updates; the last beat cuts to the wetland photo with "Every price, on the first page." Cover = the ladder still.

## Study: one fixed object while the world cuts behind it; windows that type themselves
Sheets: obrazur-reel-f8fc8ff3-frames (brand/refs/), satto-reel-84404d9d (brand/refs/).
- Obrazur: a frosted macOS-style dock with three icons sits dead centre for the whole reel. Behind it the background hard-cuts through sixteen brand worlds (green stationery, magenta fabric, a tote bag, a red mailer box, a forklift in a warehouse, a blue 3D plinth), and only the middle icon changes to that client's app icon. Hook: the dock is a familiar object in the first frame, so the eye locks on it and the cuts feel fast without being chaotic. Cut rhythm: very short holds (two frames per world in the sheet, so a fraction of a second each), one cut per beat. Camera: none, all stills. Type: none on screen, the icons are the type. Transition: hard cut, no fades. Cover: the first world with the dock. Sound: needs a beat for every cut (not in the sheet).
- Satto: three browser windows in a row on black; mono labels (CUSTOM WEBSITES, CREATIVE PORTFOLIO, FRAMER TEMPLATES) type in letter by letter beside each window and scramble out. Windows slide sideways, the copy line ("WE KEEP IT SIMPLE AND INTENTIONAL") types across the top. Camera: a slow pan. Type in and out: typewriter on, glitch off. Cover: the middle window, centred.
- Our take: one fixed object from our product (the Tuma name-check card) stays centred while the four states cut behind it on the beat, the card's footer changing each time (check, sending, sent, stopped). 6 s, hard cuts, one tick sound per cut.

## External: Kapwing video accessibility guide (2026) and Instagram's October changes (metricool.com, updated 14 Nov)
- Kapwing: captions must carry meaningful non-speech sound ("[soft click]") and be checked by hand, since auto-captions miss terms; sans-serif, a solid or semi-transparent plate behind text on busy footage.
- Metricool news log: from 28 Oct creators can no longer link reels into a series, so each reel must stand alone; carousels with music show in the Reels tab again, so our carousels should carry a quiet track; Trial Reels are capped per 30 days, so save them for real A/B tests of a cover or a hook.

## Study: a fixed numeral over cutting screens; a fixed headline with one window (Satto)
Sheets: brand/refs/satto-reel-b91bada9.jpg, brand/refs/satto-reel-6bea36ce.jpg. (The first sheet's screens include faces in the client photos; we take the structure only.)
- **Hook:** (b91bada9) the first frame is already the system: a tall dot-matrix numeral running the full height, so the cover reads as type, not as a screenshot. (6bea36ce) a serif headline split top and bottom, the product in a window between.
- **Cut rhythm:** the type never moves; only what's under or between it cuts. Each screen holds long enough to read one thing (a hero, a photo strip, a stats page), then hard-cuts.
- **Camera:** none. Flat screen captures, full bleed, no device, no tilt.
- **Type in and out:** the type is there from frame one and stays to the last; no animated entry. The numeral is the brand mark of the reel.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts inside the window; the frame around it is the constant.
- **Sound:** not in the sheets.
- **Cover:** the frame with type plus the darkest, simplest screen.
- **External (opus.pro, Instagram Reels hook formulas, vendor blog, Nov 2025):** grab attention in 1 to 2 s and pay it off by 3 s; cut the intro; on-screen text repeats the hook; "pattern then break" (three quick cuts, change on the fourth beat); a sound change at the hook. Its numbers (60% 3-second hold as the bar) are the author's own and unsourced, so we use them as a rule of thumb only. Its advice to open on a face close-up doesn't apply to us; we open on the product's one number instead.
- **For Miwa (6 s):** the drink list stays fixed on the left like Satto's type; the panel hard-cuts through four drinks on the beat, each flooding its own colour with its stock level, and the fourth beat breaks the pattern: the hibiscus count ticks from 19 to 18 with a soft pour sound.

## Study: the services list as the reel; a lit wall as the constant (WOB)
Sheets: brand/refs/wob-reel-cd59233d-frames.jpg and brand/refs/wob-reel-2d32051d-frames.jpg.
- **Hook:** (cd59233d) frame one is two lines of a list on butter cream, "01 Brand Strategy / 02 Brand Design". You read it before you know it's an ad. (2d32051d) a sunlit plaster wall with a window shadow and "WOB x" in the middle: a question waiting for a name.
- **Cut rhythm:** the list grows one line per beat to 10, then one project card per service fans in on top (sunglasses packaging, a yoga poster, a red car in snow, a sticker wall, a Carbon tray), each held about half a second, then they leave in reverse order and the list returns. The second reel alternates the empty wall with a brand collage, four times.
- **Camera:** none; flat. The wall reel fakes depth with the real window shadow.
- **Type in and out:** list lines appear without animation, one per beat; the end card types "WOB ST" then "WOB STUDIO" with a text cursor box. The wall reel ends "WOB x YOUR BRAND?".
- **Transitions:** hard cuts; cards stack with a slight rotation and a hard shadow, like prints dropped on a table.
- **Sound:** not in the sheets.
- **Cover:** the full list of ten on cream (it reads as a menu in the grid).
- **External (giovanniperilli.com/en/blog/new-instagram-features-november-2026, 24 Nov):** Instagram's Edits app got a carousel editor (8 Oct, iPhone first) that mixes photos and video on one canvas with saved font, colour and audio presets, and a free Mac version (5 Nov) with a multi-track timeline, auto captions and 4K export with no watermark. Instagram is testing 90-minute horizontal "Features" with up to eight Reels clips linked to them (26 Oct, test only). Buffer quotes Mosseri that the share of impressions going to posts under 24 hours old has grown (secondhand).
- **For us:** the WOB services reel is today's style.md "try this week". Build it in HyperFrames, then do the captions in Edits for Mac (free) and save our type and colour as presets so every reel starts from the same kit.
