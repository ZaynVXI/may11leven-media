# Memorisely (memorisely.com): what we take

A design school (Zander Whitehurst) teaching UI design, UI animation, design systems and AI product design. Its bootcamps, coaching and $99 "AI Prototyping Skills" are paid, so we don't buy them. The free parts are what we use.

## Free and useful
- **Free UI inspiration library** (memorisely.com/ui-design-inspiration): complete Figma flows (onboarding, checkout, empty states) as real files with layers, not single screens. Study whole journeys before designing an app flow, alongside Mobbin.
- **Blog:** AI prototyping, the new cadence of design work.

## Lessons for our app and web work
1. **Prototype interactive from the start,** not static click-through frames: real conditional flows, dynamic states and edge cases. Our HTML prototypes already do this; keep it the rule.
2. **Show full journeys, not single screens.** Every app concept we post shows at least empty, in-progress and success states (the "first five minutes").
3. **Keep the system in charge.** AI prototyping tools drift from a design system at scale. Figma Make keeps the library in sync, so for client app projects we build tokens and components in Figma first (our studio file), then prototype.
4. **Design engineering:** the strongest designers ship code. Our edge is real HTML, Remotion and Blender, not mockups only.
5. **Short, focused training (90 minutes) beats long courses.** It matches our daily deep-dive format.
