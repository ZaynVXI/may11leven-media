# Motto study (one entry per session, newest last)
Read before brand strategy, naming or identity work. Steal the thinking, never the surface or their trademarked phrases.

## Nomina (formerly Omni Network): one idea that points two ways
- What they did: renamed and rebranded a New York DeFi infrastructure company. The idea "All In" works twice: inside, a demand for focus and conviction; outside, an invitation to builders, investors and users to commit to open finance. Voice: driven, optimistic, approachable. Two named audiences, an early mover who needs credibility and a newcomer who needs simplicity. Rollout covered community, X campaigns and the token migration (wearemotto.com/blog/behind-the-big-idea-nomina).
- Naming: they dropped "Omni" because the word was too common to own. "Nomina" (Latin, "names") keeps the old name's letters and cadence, so existing users still recognise it.
- Visual rules: clean, professional, pragmatic; straight lines, focal points, momentum; playful looks and institutional sameness both rejected. The written case gives no typefaces or hex values.
- Why it works: one sentence does the strategy's work for staff and customers alike, so every later choice (voice, imagery, campaign) can be checked against it. The rename keeps the equity it already had instead of starting from zero.
- Also new: Motto teased a full identity for a company "rethinking how millions improve at the game they love", with 15+ sub-brands under one idea (x.com/wearemotto). Their offer is packaged as FastTrack / Foundation / Flagship (wearemotto.com/services).
- From our refs (workbook-02): the hairline grid is the cover. Title in a merged cell, one big numeral in the corner cell, one colour on near-black.
- Our move: every concept gets a two-way line, one rule for the team and one promise to the guest. For a Zanzibar hotel: "Show the room before the guest waits" (team) and "See it, price it, book it in one place" (guest). For renames in our markets, keep the letters or sound of the old name when people already use it.
