# Motto: daily study

One section per study. What they did, why it works, and one move we transform into our own work. Steal the thinking, never the surface or their trademarks.

## Attend (home concierge) and the Future of Brand report
Sources: The Brand Identity, "Building Attend: a strategic brand system shaped by Motto and AI" and "Inside the Future of Brand 2026 report with Motto's co-founders"; Motto on X (a 15+ sub-brand gaming identity teased, not yet revealed). Our refs: motto/workbook-01..03, shelf-01..10.

**What they did.** Attend means "to be present" and "to take care of". Motto built a home concierge brand around "unlocking the potential of home": a mark made of a circle (potential), a square (home) and the motion between them (unlocking); one sans serif, FT Aktual, and nothing else; a warm neutral palette with one key colour called "Sun"; three registers of the same system (app, marketing, service staff).

**Why it works.**
- Idea: the name already carries the brand promise twice, so the strategy line and the name do the same job. Nothing has to be explained.
- Type: one family. Light weights for the product, heavy weights as graphic shapes in marketing. Hierarchy comes from tint and opacity, not from a second face. This is the same rule as our workbook study (structure first), pushed into type.
- Colour: they mapped the category (blue, red, high contrast for repair and warranty) and stepped out of it on purpose. Warm neutrals plus one named colour. A named colour is easier to defend in a meeting than a hex.
- Layout: the app is light and minimal; the same system "flexes" louder for marketing and more formal for trades. One system, three volumes.
- Materials and presentation: the imagery is sunlit, lived-in rooms instead of broken pipes. Note: their library was AI-generated and then retouched. Our rule is no AI imagery, so for us the same idea means our own renders or real photography.
- Report: brand as an operating system, alignment as speed, AI as infrastructure that should fade into the experience rather than be the headline. That last point matches our rule never to lead with the tool.

**One move we transform.** The volume dial. Define one type family and one named accent per project, then write three volumes on the brand page: product (light weight, 60% tint for secondary), marketing (heavy weight as shape, accent large), service (regular, no accent). Applied today on the Kilele practice page: one family (Inter Tight) at three weights, hierarchy by tint only, Infrared used on the active unit and nowhere else.
