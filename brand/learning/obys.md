# Obys: the bar for showing a website

Obys is a concept-driven studio in the EU (brand, web, 3D, creative direction). "Each project becomes a case study." Refs: brand/refs/obys/.

## What one Kopetzki post contains (one carousel)
1. **Studio Display, front-on, grey sweep, black desk band.** The screen plays a real interaction for 44 s: hero, scroll, numbers counting up (500+, 10+, 1), sticky "How We Help" header, cards turning blue on hover. Calm, no cuts.
2. **Phone leaning in a dark studio** on a perforated dot-grid surface, one spot light, a soft reflection. Real titanium phone, real glass.
3. **MacBook on perforated metal**, black room, one strip of light.
4. **Single page sections, flat, full width,** on one solid field (navy #11203A or blue-grey #A7B1BF). No device, just the crisp page. Each is one idea: How We Help, Access to Justice, How It Works, Beyond Winning, 404.
5. **Page-wall reel (10 s):** three columns of full page sections that flip like cubes, revealing the next page. Shows the whole system at once: 15+ distinct pages.

## Why it looks expensive (and ours doesn't yet)
- **The site itself is rich.** 15+ pages: hero, process, tools, glossary, map ("Geography of Work"), founder, approach, 404. Ours had one page.
- **One motif runs everywhere.** Horizontal stripes from the logo close every page, the 404 and the phone hero. Ours: the sail, used only as a logo.
- **Type is huge and confident.** Page titles at about 8% of the page width, tight; body small. Big numbers as content (500+ / 10+ / 1).
- **Numbered cards with hairlines** (01, 02… a dot at the top right, a footer line with a short action and an arrow). One state in blue to show interaction.
- **Photos are atmosphere.** Motion-blurred city shots behind cards, never stock-literal.
- **Devices are real 3D renders** in a dark, controlled studio: one light, real reflections, materials from the brand's mood (perforated metal for a precise law firm).
- **Motion is the product:** counters, hovers, sticky headers, flips. Recorded from the real build, not faked.
- **Every slide is one frame, one idea,** on one background colour per post.

## Our rules from this
1. Build the site properly first (8+ pages or sections, a motif, real content) before presenting it. Presentation can't rescue a thin page.
2. Each post mixes three kinds of slide: a flat page on a solid field, a real device render, and one motion piece.
3. Devices come from Blender (brand/concepts/*/device/) or measured stock, lit in a set that matches the brand's material. Never a flat screenshot with a drop shadow.
4. Record real interactions (Transitions.dev motion, counters, hovers) with Playwright inside the device.
5. One motif from the identity runs through every page and every slide.
6. Steal like an artist: never stripes, never their navy and blue cards. Take the thinking: a system, rich pages, real devices, recorded motion.

## The Obys site itself (recorded: brand/refs/obys/site/)
- A preloader that is itself a collage of their work, counting to 100.
- Work index = one vertical film strip of project images in the centre, a bracket "( )" that frames the current one, the project list in light grey at the left, the name and disciplines in small type beside the bracket. Switch views: Vertical, Horizontal, Grid.
- Almost no words on the homepage: a 4-line studio statement, an email, the local time. The work speaks.
- Case studies are immersive: full-bleed dark pages that behave like the client's product (Porsche: the car, the charger, "Take charge").
