# Application development: what's current
- Show apps as recorded prototypes with real states (default, pressed, selected, loading, success), local payments and a status bar that matches the device.
- Platform patterns: bottom sheets, step progress, tab bars, one big numeral for the number that matters.

## Current (apps deep-dive, Fri)
- African super-apps are the frame, not single-purpose apps: Vodacom/M-PESA reports 221 mini apps and 9.4m active financial-services app users, +40% YoY; Safaricom's My OneApp merges M-PESA and MySafaricom into one entry point (tech-ish.com, vodacom FY2026). The design lesson: the ecosystem expands around existing habits - users "don't need to learn a new system". So a concept in our markets should look like a mini app that could sit INSIDE M-PESA, not a rival super-app: one job, M-Pesa checkout native, minimal new vocabulary.
- Gulf localisation (trustradio 2026 guide): don't blind-mirror for RTL - flip directional icons (arrows, progress) but keep clocks, checkmarks, search and media controls LTR; hierarchy starts top-right. Arabic script is dense: +~10% font size and more line-height. Support local rails (Mada in KSA, KNET in Kuwait, Fawran/Apple Pay in Qatar), not just Visa/Mastercard. Adjust timing for Ramadan and prayer times. (Some stats in the guide are uncited - treat as direction, not fact.)
- iOS 26 Liquid Glass is a cautionary note, not a trend to chase: NN/g says it "prioritises spectacle over usability" - restless, less legible, "a fogged-up window". For our bar (usable + beautiful), keep surfaces opaque where text sits, reserve translucency for chrome that doesn't carry reading. Mita uses flat opaque surfaces and one solid accent block.
- Africa angle: the opaque-bill problem. KPLC prepaid tokens buy different units for the same money - a unit is ~KES 12, "about 16 with taxes", and up to 20% of a purchase can clear arrears first (peopledaily, kenyans.co.ke). A meter app that SHOWS the split (energy / taxes / arrears) turns a grudge errand into understanding. That's the Mita concept.
- Gulf angle: the same transparency idea reskinned - a DEWA/utility top-up that shows the slab tariff you've crossed this month, RTL-aware, Apple Pay one-tap.
- Rest of world: the "mini app inside a wallet" pattern is global (WeChat, now M-PESA) - design concepts as embeddable flows with one numeral, one action, native pay.
