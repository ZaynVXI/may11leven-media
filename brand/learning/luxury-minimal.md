# Luxury, still minimal (from Zayn's "Luxury branding trends 2027" video)

Reference sheet: `brand/refs/reels/luxury-trends-2027-sheet.jpg` (a creator talking about trends; never use the presenter's face). Zayn's ask: brand us and our work in a luxurious way, but still minimal, because we're a premium studio.

## The five trends it names (on-screen text)
1. **Y2K editorial minimalism.** Work that feels like a Vogue spread from the 2000s: serif headlines, product on a plain field, magazine page layouts and fragrance-ad restraint (Chanel, a perfume bottle on pale blue). "Kitsch" is used carefully, as one playful detail.
2. **Clean, nostalgic, editorial photography.** "We're tired of maximalism and clashing colours." Less flash, real skin, real texture, film-like warmth, and objects shot like still lifes (a glass on a burnt-orange gradient).
3. **Pattern revival.** "The mix between patterns and gradients", retro-futuristic modern, and "the classic quality of a pattern": checks, blurred stripes and soft gradient boxes on packaging.
4. **Baby blue / powder blue.** "The colour luxury keeps reaching for right now" (Loewe bottle, a powder-blue set, a blue knit).
5. **The visible human touch.** Handwritten notes (an Acne Studios-style handwritten invitation), paper cut-outs, objects made by hand. "The trend is proof that a person made it, and not a machine."

## What this means for May11Leven (transformed, never copied)
- **Our luxury is restraint plus one human trace.** Keep Motto's discipline (one object, air, a system), and add a sign of the hand: a handwritten note in a reel, a pencil mark on a construction grid, a folded paper card, a real fingerprint on glass in Blender.
- **Editorial layouts:** a serif display moment (Instrument Serif italic is already in our kit) against a neo-grotesk, magazine-style captions, generous margins, page numbers. Studio cards can read like a fashion-magazine spread.
- **Photography direction:** calm, warm, film-like, no flash. Use free stock that passes this test only.
- **Colour:** powder blue (try #BFD3E6 or #C9D9E8) as a seasonal accent world for selected posts and concepts (fragrance, hospitality, real estate), never replacing our base. Use it as a field colour, not as text on light grounds.
- **Pattern:** one restrained pattern-plus-gradient texture per project (packaging, brand-book dividers), never as wallpaper.
- **Our anti-AI edge:** this trend matches our no-AI-imagery rule. We can say so honestly in captions about process ("drawn by hand first").

## Luxury checklist (add to Pass 0 for premium posts)
- One hero, lots of air, and nothing shouting.
- One editorial type moment (a serif or a big italic word), the rest quiet.
- A real material with real light, and one human trace.
- A colour world of at most 3 tones, one of them possibly the season's accent.
- Would it sit in a Vogue or fragrance spread? Would it sit next to Motto? It needs both answers to be yes.
