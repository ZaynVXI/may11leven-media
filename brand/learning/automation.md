# AI automation: what's current
- WhatsApp is the front door for small businesses; AI agents triage support, qualify leads, collect details, send status updates and run approvals inside WhatsApp (jestor, betanews)
- Results businesses report: fewer abandoned chats, more resolved without a human, full history logged in their system (jestor)
- No-code agent builders let SMEs launch without developers; the value is in the flow design and the brand voice (callmissed)

- Lead with local reality: M-Pesa/MoMo payment links, Swahili/English/French replies, opening hours, delivery status.
