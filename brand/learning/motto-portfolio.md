# How Motto thinks: portfolio, case studies and process

Sources: wearemotto.com/work (17 projects), /portfolio/outten-golden, /flagship, /what-is-a-successful-rebranding-strategy, the Mindsparkle feature on Ivo, and casestudies.com summaries (Zenarate, Cresta, Protégé, AMP Z, IA Interior Architects, Blix, Blue Sky Studios).

## The problem they always solve
The same problem, in every industry: **the brand hasn't caught up with the business.**
- Outten & Golden: "Albert Einstein walking around in a bathrobe". Brilliant firm, brand that didn't show it.
- Ivo: the product was used by Reddit, Shopify, Uber and Atlassian, but the brand looked like an earlier stage. It was going after bigger rounds and bigger deals.
- Zenarate: grew from one product into a platform. It needed a new category story without losing what it already had.
- Cresta, AMP Z, Protégé: a moment of growth (a Series B or D, going upmarket) where the brand has to look as big as the company is becoming.
The trigger is always a **defining moment**: funding, a move upmarket, a merger, a new category.

## How they think
- "A new logo won't fix weak positioning." Strategy comes before visuals, every time.
- "Every successful rebrand is built on insight, not assumptions." Each project starts with immersion: stakeholder interviews, brand and competitor audits, the client's own history (O&G's landmark cases), and workshops.
- **Find the tensions, then resolve them in one phrase.** O&G was personal vs professional, approachable vs elite, boutique vs powerhouse. That became "approachable sophistication".
- **One Idea Worth Rallying Around** (their trademarked method). One line the whole brand hangs on: O&G "It's always personal." Ivo "Intelligence by design." Zenarate "Close the loop."
- **Verbal identity counts as much as visual.** A named voice persona (O&G's is "Hope", compassionate yet fierce), three voice pillars (Empathetic, Determined, Clear) and signature phrases ("A guiding light").
- **Visuals are argued, not just shown:** the ampersand stands for connection, serif plus sans means expertise plus modernity, the palette means optimism, trust and warmth. Ivo uses illustration and a warm palette to stand apart from cold, generic tech.
- **Activation makes it real:** photoshoots in two cities, custom 3D, line illustrations to explain complex ideas, swag packs, the website and its copy.

## How a case study page is built (O&G)
Overview → Challenge → Brand immersion → Brand strategy → Verbal identity → Visual identity → Testimonial → Brand activation.
They show the thinking first, the look second, then the proof and the rollout. The social posts then run the project as a series, one section at a time.

## How they sell (Flagship)
- **The page opens with the pain:** "When the business changes, the brand should too."
- **A self-check list:** you've outgrown your brand, your teams tell different stories, the market sees you differently than leadership does.
- **Three outcomes:** One direction. One story. One standard.
- **A sequence:** immerse → who you're becoming → why people should care → how you sound → how you look → how you bring it to life. It takes 16–24 weeks.
- **They ask for commitment:** a steering group, an executive sponsor and a named project advocate.
- **Proof:** client logos, testimonials and a few numbers. No price shown. The ask is "Book a discovery call".

## What we take (transformed for our market)
1. **Our trigger moments:** a first funding round, opening a second branch, moving from Instagram selling to a real shop, entering the Gulf, a tourism or real-estate launch. Post about the moment, not the deliverable.
2. **Our method gets a name and a sequence:** Listen (immersion) → One Line (the sentence the brand rallies around) → Voice → Look → Build (site, app, assistant) → Launch. Every concept we post shows at least the One Line and the reasoning behind the visuals.
3. **Tensions, then one phrase.** Every concept brief lists 3 tensions and the phrase that resolves them, before any design.
4. **A voice persona and pillars** are part of the brand package (Mark).
5. **Case-study pages follow their order**, and each section becomes one post in a series.
6. **The sales page follows the Flagship structure:** pain → self-check → 3 outcomes → sequence → proof → one call to action. Ours shows the price, because the panels keep asking for it and our buyers are smaller businesses.
7. **We won't copy:** their ® names, the "Idea Worth Rallying Around" wording or their phrasing. The thinking is what we take.
