# Pulse: what's new (trim old lines; max ~40)
## Merch and packaging
- Merch 2026: limited runs and collectable editions replace giveaways; blind/mystery boxes, mini collectables, region-specific runs, textured packaging, single-tone neutral palettes, teal as a key colour (insights.fluidbranding.com)
- Packaging: Hwila mineral water (Sweden, Neumeister) modernised for a younger audience in blue + white on aluminium, glass and PET - a heritage brand made calm, one colour (thedieline.com)
- China: plain illustration and subtle sketched packs stand out in a loud marketplace (trendhunter.com); see packaging-merch.md for guochao notes.
## Marketing (Thursday deep dive: see marketing.md)
- Instagram "Your Algorithm": users can add/remove interest topics that shape Reels recommendations, rolled out to all English-speaking users (thekeyword.co). Topic-clear captions and on-screen text matter more.
- Reels get most of their life early: days 1-3 = ~65% of Reel views; ~70% of all views land in 72 h (blog.mean.ceo citing Metricool 2026 study).
- Accounts under 10k: reach per follower Reels 4.9%, images 3.5%, carousels 2.6%; images still have the best interaction rate (0.51% vs 0.34%) (fanpagekarma.com).
## Markets
- Kenya: highest social time in the world (~27 h/week), TikTok 18.4M, WhatsApp 97%, social commerce 65%, Snapchat +39% y/y. Nigeria: TikTok 47.8M adults, social commerce 69%, ARCON pre-approval needed for sponsored content aimed at Nigerians (also from abroad). South Africa: Instagram 8.6M users growing 19.4% y/y, WhatsApp 96% (kolsquare.com)
- UAE: LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook lead for business; short video, social commerce and original Arabic content are the 2026 trends (redberries.ae)
