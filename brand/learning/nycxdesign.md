# NYCxDESIGN (nycxdesign.org)

New York's official design week, run by a non-profit, with programming all year and a festival across architecture, public space, product, technology and entertainment.

- **Next festival:** May 13–20, 2027. The 2026 archive is the study material (festival calendar).
- **Partners:** Adobe, Lexus, ICFF, Cornell Tech, Santiago Calatrava, INC Architects. Media partners: Dezeen, Metropolis, Architizer, The Architect's Newspaper.
- **Awards:** an awards programme exists under the festival brand (run with Interior Design / Sandow). Check its categories, fees and deadlines on the official festival pages before planning an entry; most entries are paid, so it's Zayn's call.

## Why it matters to us
- **A study source:** product, object and spatial design, close to our packaging and 3D work and to the real estate and hospitality market (East Africa lodges, Gulf developments).
- **Content moment:** a "what we saw at design week" story series in May (from the online guide and press coverage, credited, no faces).
- **Long-term goal:** an award entry once we have real client work, and a world design-week moment for an African studio angle.
