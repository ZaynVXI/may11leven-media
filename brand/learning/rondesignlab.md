# Ron Design Lab: what we take

## Facts (Clutch, DesignRush; their claims are marked as claims)
- Kraków, founded 2015, 10–49 people. Mostly UX/UI and product design, with some web.
- **Industries:** real estate (~30%) and financial services (~20%). Real estate overlaps our lead niche.
- **35 Clutch reviews at 4.9.** Clients praise communication, deadlines, organisation and flexibility more often than visuals.
- Most projects fall between $10k and $50k.
- Self-reported: 500+ projects, 80M+ views, clients raised $120M+. Clients listed include Samsung, Colgate and Ford, plus health apps (Soma, Veri) featured on Abduzeedo.

## Lessons
1. **Concept shots as a lead engine.** Polished UI concepts for real problems (health, fintech, property), published on Behance, Dribbble and Instagram. Views turn into inbound work. That's our Flagship and concept route, done with honest labels.
2. **Reviews are the proof that sells.** Clients buy reliability (replies, deadlines, updates) as much as taste. When we have clients, ask for reviews on Clutch and Google, and say plainly in our own channels how we work: a weekly update, fixed dates.
3. **Getting into the design press** (Abduzeedo, Behance features) gives credibility at no cost. Submit our best concept case studies there once they pass the Motto test.
4. **A narrow, valuable focus** (real estate, fintech, health) makes the portfolio read as expertise.
