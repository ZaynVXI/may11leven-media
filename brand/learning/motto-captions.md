# How Motto writes captions (and how we use it)

Reference: `brand/refs/reels/motto-future-branding/` (a word-drum reel of Future, Branding, Tech, Robotics and Lead, with the caption screenshot).

## The reel
- One idea, pure type: giant black grotesk on off-white. Words turn on a drum, and the neighbours squash and fade. About 3 seconds, looping, no logo and no CTA in the video.

## The caption pattern
1. **A tension line first:** "Most tech founders spend years building breakthrough technology." It names the reader without saying "you".
2. **The turn:** "Very few spend enough time building the story that helps people believe in it."
3. **The stakes, in four words:** "And that gap is expensive."
4. **Short paragraphs, one idea each.** Contrasts that move: "Investors fund innovation. They follow vision."
5. **A closing three-beat line:** "Capital follows conviction. Clarity attracts it. Consistency sustains it." This is the only place a three-part rhythm is used.
6. **Credit and source:** "Read the full article by @sunnybonnell", the article title, "link in bio". It sells thinking, not a service, and there are no hashtags.

## Our rules from it
- Thought-leadership posts open with a tension line about the reader's world (a closing time, a phone, a payment), never about us.
- One idea per paragraph, and at most one three-beat line per caption.
- The service mention comes late and once. On LinkedIn, no "comment 11".
- When we quote someone, we credit them by name. Real sources only.

Our first version is `posts/night-shift/` ("Sell / Book / Reply / Get paid / Grow ... while you sleep.").
