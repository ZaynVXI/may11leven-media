# Miwa research (practice concept, not a client)

Problem and user: a city juice bar's order page. Office workers in Nairobi CBD order at lunch from a phone or a desk. The questions they'd otherwise have to ask: what's fresh today, how much, how big, do you deliver to me, how do I pay. Most delivery listings show a name and a price and nothing about what's in the glass or when it was made.

Feeling (3 words): fresh, honest, quick.

Category screens studied:
- Smoothie DJS on Uber Eats (Lower State House Road, Nairobi): every fresh juice is KES 250 (apple, tamarind, pineapple, passion, hibiscus blend, pineapple mint, mango, sugarcane with turmeric or mint, watermelon lemonade). No sizes shown anywhere. Lesson: the price is flat, so the menu can lead with the drinks, not the prices; the missing size is the gap to fill.
- Fit Juice Bar Cafe on Uber Eats (Peony Estate): menu split into fresh juices, herbal remedies, fruit salads; items load by script and show nothing on a slow phone. Lesson: the first screen should work as plain HTML.
- PXDX NOAL (dribbble.com/shots/25039049): the legal gate (16+) is a small toast inside the hero, written in the bar's voice ("Chill, I'm 16+"), and the palette is sampled from the scene (tail-light red, racing green).

Award/gallery sites studied:
- godly.website (Recent): the feed is now single interactions (Payment Swipe, Cinema Seat Preview, Moody Status Badges). One component state is enough to carry a post.
- onepagelove.com/hoy: a scroll-controlled demo recreates the product's real interaction instead of a screenshot.
- onepagelove.com/soma: the product's own data drawn as the illustration (voice patterns as botanical lines).
- onepagelove.com/solea: near-black canvas, pixel waveforms, two restrained accents.
- minimal.gallery (home): studio sites (Studio Plato, OPENINGHOURS Studio) built on an index list and almost no imagery.
- zeroframe.framer.website (Satto): one weight (500), a 12 px mono label at +0.04em, zero-padded counters (012, 010, 016), lowercase nav.

Zayn's references used:
- holographik-13: a price ladder where one value is lifted and the rest are set back by tone.
- satto-07: index list plus one preview; the selected row swaps the preview.
- motto workbook-02: the structure is the cover; a big numeral in a corner cell.

Visual language: Bright consumer, kept minimal (saturated flat colour, rounded shapes, one field at a time). Last piece (Ikiyaga) was warm editorial on a photo, so this one has no photo at all.

Colour/material source: the drinks themselves. Each juice has its own measured colour, and the selected drink floods the panel. The ground is cane pulp, the ink is tamarind.

Type and why: Bricolage Grotesque (OFL, in brand/fonts/library). Its optical size axis gives a soft, inky display at large sizes and a plain text cut small; friendly without being a rounded "kids" face. DM Mono for the measured data (ml, KES, minutes), because those are readings.

Truth notes: KES 250 per glass is the real Nairobi CBD price (Smoothie DJS). The 350 ml glass is our concept size; no listing we found states one. The delivery radius and time are concept values and are labelled as such in the footer.

Steal list:
1. PXDX NOAL: the required notice (cookie consent under Kenya's Data Protection Act) becomes a small toast in the hero in the shop's voice, with "No" as easy as "Yes". Ours differs: it carries a real legal choice, not a joke gate, and it never covers a control (WCAG 2.4.11).
2. Holographik price ladder + Satto index: the menu is the hero list; one row lifted drives the big panel.
3. Motto workbook: one big numeral in the panel corner (the glass's ml), the grid carries the rest.

Avoid list: fruit photos with splashes, leaf illustrations, green "healthy" gradients, emoji, a "Detox" claim, a cookie bar pinned across the bottom.
