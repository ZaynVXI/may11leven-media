# Ikiyaga Residences research (practice concept, Kigali)

Problem and user: Kigali buyers (local professionals, diaspora buying from abroad) can't see what a flat costs. Listings say "From 374,000,000 RWF, check brochure" (The ONE Residence, Kimihurura, plutproperties.com) and hide per-unit prices behind a call. The diaspora buyer at 23:00 in Brussels has nobody to call.
Feeling (3 words): calm, informed, sure.

Category studied:
- toimi.pro best real-estate sites: 111 West 57th is the only one with a unit selector and a stated price range; Compass leaves colour to the photos (black/white UI); Airbnb shows the total price up front. Lesson: a visible price is the rarest and most trusted thing in the category.
- Plut Properties / The ONE Residence: 1 bed 91 m², 2 bed 144–155 m², 3 bed 214 m², 30% deposit, "from" price only. Gives our price truth: about RWF 4.1M per m² in Kimihurura; we priced the wetland side lower, about RWF 2.9–3.2M per m².
- Webflow's 2026 trends: minimalism in copy (Ruul), the TL;DR experience (Flabbergast). Lesson: the whole offer on one screen.
- Orbix trends: bento grids add noise to a page with one message; skip.

Zayn's references used:
- holographik-13: a price ladder, one selected value in a blue block, the rest fading with distance. The list of numbers is the image.
- lebedev-14 (Haven): one ink plus the photo's colour; the place carries the palette.
- satto-07: index list with one inverted row, nothing else decorates.
- motto/workbook-02: the structure is the cover.
- nuevo-03: the number is the image.

Visual language: Warm editorial turned cool (real photo of the place, one ink, a list as hero). Different from the last piece (Mita: an LCD utility app with an owned accent block).
Colour/material source: the sky over Nyandungu (#C3D2DC family, ground #E8EDF0) and the reeds (#586B3E, darkened to #3E4E2A for the selected row; ink #1B2416).
Type: Hanken Grotesk, one weight (Regular), tabular numerals. The serif was cut after the cream + serif check.

Steal list:
1. Holographik: the price list is the hero, one value lifted, the rest set back by tone. Ours: tone means status (available full ink, reserved and sold set back, sold also struck through).
2. Lebedev: colour from the place, not from a palette. Ours: the wetland sky and reeds.
3. Satto: one inverted row, nothing else. Ours: the inverted row drives the detail strip (deposit, balance, per m²).

Avoid list: "From X, call us" pricing; status pills in three colours; glass tags on the hero photo; render-of-the-building heroes; two equal CTAs.
