# Coach seat picker research (concept, not a client)

Problem and user: on the Nairobi to Mombasa train, you pay first and the coach and seat number arrive afterwards in the confirmation message. Group bookings are not accepted. A family of four can pay for four tickets and still sit apart. Source: Kenya Railways FAQ, metickets.krc.co.ke/faqs.php (items 7, 8, 10). Fares from the same FAQ: Economy KES 1,500, First KES 4,500, Premium KES 12,000 one way. Departures 8:00 am, 3:00 pm and 10:00 pm from both ends. Payment by M-Pesa (SIM toolkit) or card at the counter.
User: a parent booking four Economy seats on a phone, the night before, who wants to know where the family will sit before paying.
Concept values (not real data): the coach layout (2 + 3 seats across, 16 rows), which seats are taken, and the coach number. The tile says "concept layout".

Feeling (3 words): sure, together, calm.

Category screens studied:
- PXDX Tennis Courts Reservation App (dribbble.com/shots/24603734): the court picker is a map of the real club (A and C indoor, B outdoor); the selected court is a lifted tile with a check; the deposit rule is written under the price.
- Airline seat maps in general (rows lettered, the aisle as a gap): the map is the picker.
Award and gallery sites studied (from today's study, see awwwards.md and trends.md): Minh Pham portfolio SOTD (one motion vocabulary), fromanother SOTD (one reveal grammar), Lovable 2026 trends guide (organic shapes, anti-grid), Icons8 Dribbble trends (monochrome palettes, broken grids; undated), Dribbble Select 2026 (calm, structured products).
Zayn's references used:
- brand/refs/pxdx-tennis/01-tennis.jpg: the picker is a map of the real place.
- brand/refs/motto/ workbook: the hairline grid is the cover, one big numeral.
- brand/refs/wob-reel-cd59233d-frames.jpg: a numbered list where one line is lit at a time.

Visual language: Swiss data (strict grid, black on paper, one accent, huge numerals). The last piece, Miwa, was Bright consumer.
Colour: paper #F1EFE8, ink #121212, grey #6B6A66, one accent from the line's own ground: Tsavo red earth #B4462B.
Type: Hanken Grotesk (neutral, tabular figures, Swiss tone) 400/600, Red Hat Mono 400 for seat codes and fares.
Steal list:
1. The picker is a map of the place (PXDX tennis). Ours: the coach itself, seats you can count.
2. One big numeral carries the answer (Motto workbook). Ours: "4", the family, together.
3. One thing lit at a time (WOB services reel). Ours: one row lit in the accent, the rest ink lines.
Avoid list: a dropdown for class, a seat list with no map, gradients, a "Book now" button that hides the total, stock photos of trains.
