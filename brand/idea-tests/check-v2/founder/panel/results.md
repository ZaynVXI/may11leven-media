## What would flip a no

- A named client, or a live trial on my own till data showing it catches a fake payment before I release goods, with the results shown to me first.
- A working demo I can test on my own till data, plus a named local partner or a clear same-day support promise.
- A clear rebrand offer for a business like mine, with examples of their past work and a fixed price.
- A named local shop or business that uses it every day, with real numbers from their own till, and a way for me to try it on my own M-Pesa feed before I pay.
- A short example using the card or booking system I already run, showing the exact money it would have caught last month, with a clear price I could cancel in a month.
- A free trial on my own till and payments, with a clear monthly price I can cancel before I pay anything.
- A live product with a free trial, a named M-Pesa integration, and a demo run on a real till that shows the reply in two seconds.
- A live product with a named case from a business like mine, showing it works on my own payment or lead data, and a clear price before I have to book a call.
- A free trial on my own till that shows it matching real payments correctly, with a clear monthly price before I hand over any details.
- A live case from a business like mine, with real numbers, and a local contact I can call if the agent gets a payment wrong.
