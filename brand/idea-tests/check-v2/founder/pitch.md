An Instagram carousel from May11Leven Labs, a design studio, showing a concept (labelled "concept, not a live product") for its AI agents service.
Cover: a shop counter in Nairobi, a customer's phone showing an M-Pesa "payment received" SMS for KES 8,200. Headline: "He showed you the payment. It never arrived."
Slide 2: the problem. Shops, landlords and delivery riders hand over goods after seeing a fake M-Pesa confirmation, and find out at closing time.
Slide 3: how the agent works: it watches your own till's real payments (from your M-Pesa business statement feed), and when a customer says "I've paid", your staff type the code into WhatsApp and get a one-line answer in two seconds: "Received, KES 8,200, from J. Mwangi, 14:32" or "Not received. Don't release the goods."
Slide 4: at closing time it sends the owner one message: today's real takings, any mismatches, and who served them.
Slide 5: "Your business has a question like this every day. We design agents that check, sort and answer it for you." Comment 11 for a free agent task map for your business.
