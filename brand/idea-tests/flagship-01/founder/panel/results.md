## What would flip a no

- A guesthouse like mine showing me real direct bookings coming in each month, with the figures, before I spend anything.
- A real before-and-after from a guesthouse like mine, with its own commission and booking numbers, showing the direct share rising and what it cost to set up, plus proof that the booking engine I already pay for can take the new page without a second subscription.
- Proof from my own booking engine that it cannot take a deposit and send the day-before reminder, plus a free look at my real commission numbers from last season that shows the saving is real.
- Our own commission figures run through the calculator, plus a reference from a hotel of similar size that moved bookings to direct and kept them there after a year.
- A named hotel of our size, with real before-and-after figures for direct bookings and commission, that I can phone and check, plus a setup that works with our current booking engine and does not cost us visibility on Booking.com.
- Seeing my own numbers: how many of my bookings are repeat or referral guests who would book direct anyway, and a written promise that Booking.com stays on as the main channel while the direct page only takes the repeat guests.
- Seeing a finished booking page from a guesthouse on my own coast, with the owner willing to tell me how it went and how many no-shows it stopped.
- Seeing a guesthouse like mine actually get direct bookings from the Instagram link, with the numbers, plugged into the SiteMinder or Cloudbeds system I already use so I don't have to rebuild anything.
- Named East African or Gulf hotels they have already set up, with the owners willing to talk to me and real before-and-after booking numbers.
- A real guesthouse like mine that has used this setup for a few months, with the direct bookings and commission saved shown in its own numbers.
