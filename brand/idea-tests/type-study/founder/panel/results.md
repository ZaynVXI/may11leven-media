# Buyer panel: May11Leven Labs, a small design studio (brand identity, websites, apps, 3D, automation, AI agents, content) that markets itself on Instagram

**5 buy · 15 pass** (25% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 311161, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

(P004) · "I already have a designer and I am not going to swap one for a stranger off a carousel. ## What would flip a no

- A before-and-after from a business like mine, showing more bookings or sales after the rebrand.
- Real brand work for businesses like mine (a hotel, clinic or real estate firm) with named clients and a result they can point to.
- A clear price range for a full identity, and a before-and-after from a business like ours, such as a hotel or a clinic, that shows the result we'd get.
- A clear, published price range for a brand identity, plus proof that the studio has done a project for a business like ours at a cost lower than what we pay our designer now.
- A handful of named clients in my sector, with real before-and-after results and a local contact I can call or visit.
- Case studies of real brands it has built for hotels, real estate, restaurants or fashion, with the results they got.
- A case study of a brand like ours, showing the before, the after and what changed in bookings or sales.
- A slide that shows a real business like mine before and after, with what changed in bookings or enquiries, and a plain price range on the first post.
- Seeing two or three named businesses like mine, with their before and after brand work and a result they can point to, shown in the feed.
- Named client work with before and after results, such as bookings or sales figures, from businesses like mine in hotels, clinics or real estate.
- Seeing a finished brand from a business like mine, my town or my kind of trade, with the price stated up front.
- A case study from a business like mine, showing the before and after numbers, such as more enquiries or bookings, not just the finished design.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
