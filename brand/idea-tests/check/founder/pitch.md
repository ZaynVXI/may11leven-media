An Instagram carousel from May11Leven Labs, a design studio, showing a concept (clearly labelled "concept, not a live product") for its AI agents service.
Cover: a phone in a hand on a matatu seat. On screen, a WhatsApp chat called "Check". Headline: "I sent you KES 4,500 by mistake. Please send it back."
Slide 2: the problem. Fake M-Pesa "wrong transfer" SMS look exactly like real ones, and people send money back that was never sent.
Slide 3: how it works in three steps: forward the message to Check on WhatsApp; it compares it with known scam patterns and asks you to check your real M-Pesa balance; it answers in one line ("This is a known scam. Your balance has not changed. Don't send anything.").
Slide 4: three chat states: Scam, Looks real, Not sure (call Safaricom on 100).
Slide 5: "Your business has questions like this every day. We build agents that read every message, sort it and draft the answer for you to approve." Comment 11 for a free agent task map for your business.
