# Check: judgement brief (concept, AI agents, 06 Kelp)

- **Problem:** People in Kenya send money back after fake "I sent you KES 4,500 by mistake" SMS, because the fake text looks exactly like a real M-Pesa message.
- **Idea:** forward the text to Check on WhatsApp and get a one-line answer before you send anything.
- **Hero:** one phone showing the Check chat with the verdict "Known scam. Your balance hasn't changed. Don't send anything."
- **Surface and set:** a worn vinyl matatu seat, the phone lying on it, with a window-light stripe across the seat. Free stock plate, or a Blender seat.
- **Light:** low late-afternoon sun through a bus window from the left, hard edge, warm.
- **Lens and framing:** 50 mm, top-down 3/4, phone in the lower third, 55% empty seat.
- **Human trace:** a folded paper bus ticket next to the phone.
- **Grid slot:** device scene, warm. The next tile is dark (process rail) and the one after is light (three states).
- **Palette (project's own):** matatu-seat oxblood #5A1F1E, phone-glass ink, WhatsApp-neutral chat paper #EFEAE2, and one signal: verdict red #D93A2B for scam and green #2F7D4F for real. Kelp #1F5E5A appears only as the small service marker.
- **Axon lessons:** problem headline, one process rail (01 Forward · 02 Compare · 03 Answer) drawn isometrically, real numbers in the UI (KES 4,500, balance KES 12,380), and a task CTA ("Forward your first message").
