# Motion references (the training set for our motion skill)
PXDX trained their Claude with three reference videos plus one sentence: "Create a skill named [name] for Figma Motion. The motion should be calm and smooth." Then every refinement ended with "Add to the skill: …" so the fix applied to all future animations.

Ours:
- reference-1-pill-to-card.mp4, reference-2-widget-wall.mp4, reference-3-player.mp4: PXDX experiments, study only, never posted.
- ours-lab01.mp4: our first Figma Motion piece.
Brief: the motion should be calm and smooth; one thing moves at a time; glows and light stay subtle.
Add a new reference here whenever Zayn sends one he likes, and replace weak ones with our own best pieces as we make them.
