# Editorial layouts on a phone (idea bank)

1. **Name in the top third.** Big, tight type, set across two lines with a step ("Vale / Works ©").
2. **Two columns even on a phone:** a small label on the left ("My practice"), short text on the right.
3. **Small paragraphs, uppercase blocks:** a short statement in caps, line by line ("Light across a wall. A table that gathers.").
4. **One-word page titles large enough to find the page at a glance** ("Contact"), with a B&W photo and the details below it.
5. **Full-screen menu:** large items stacked with hairlines between them, the email small, the logo huge at the bottom.

Where it fits us: the Kusi House mobile pages, our own site's menu and contact page, and the calculator on phones (label column already matches rule 2).

# Four layers (idea bank, from a second reel)
A landing page shown at zero design, then four layers added one by one, each named on a small card:
1. Colour and type: black, one accent (acid lime), a mono plus an italic serif word ("The handoffs run *themselves.*").
2. Depth: light behind (a WebGL light stream), frosted glass panels.
3. Motion: the stream flows.
4. All four together, then the scroll.
CTA "Comment LAYERS for the prompt". Our version: screen-only, our own concept site, the four cards in our type, CTA "Comment 11".

# Resource carousel (idea bank, from a post Zayn shared)
"Bookmark these: UI / visual design inspiration sites". Cover is a moving grid of shots. Each slide: a screenshot of the site on a warm off-white field, a huge number in one accent colour bottom right, the site name large in a second colour. Last slide is the "bonus". Built to be saved. Our version: "Where we look before we design". Slides 1–2 are the two studios we study most, Motto and Artemii Lebedev (their own sites, credited, with what we learn from each in one line). Then the galleries (Refero, Land-book, Godly, Minimal Gallery, Siteinspire, Mobbin), are.na/fi as the bonus. Our type, Infrared numbers, last slide Comment 11. Use only screenshots we take of public pages, credit every site, never present their work as ours.

# Landing-page carousel (idea bank, Orbix Studio "Nexfi" post Zayn shared)
**What works**
- Cover: the page upright inside a soft rounded frame on light grey, with a fixed signature bar at the bottom (avatar, "Designed by", like/comment/share icons, "Save for later"). The same bar on every post makes the series recognisable.
- Inside slides: the full desktop page **rotated 90°** so a wide page fills a tall 4:5 slide; people turn the phone or zoom, and the whole page is shown large, section by section.
- Last slide: a contact card (big line "Let's discuss…", "Book a free 15-min call", a WhatsApp QR code, handles).
- Caption: title line, then **one design decision explained** ("Right after the about line, the page stops talking and shows numbers"), then "What we designed" as a list, then the CTA.

**Where we beat it**
- Their copy is generic ("Transforming Businesses With AI & Innovation"), with typos ("Get Start Free", "Monthly Expanse"), placeholder testimonials ("John Doe") and invented stats with real people's faces. Ours: real, specific copy, honest numbers or clearly labelled concept figures, no faces.
- One accent, one idea per section, our type.

**Our version**
- Use the rotated full-page slides for every concept website (Kusi House, Direct, Consent): cover upright in the client's world, 3–5 rotated sections, last slide our "Comment 11" card with the emails (a QR only to a public link Zayn approves).
- Our own signature bar, designed once, never theirs: May11Leven 11 mark, "Concept by May11Leven Labs", the service name.
- Caption: one design decision explained, then what we designed, then the CTA.
