Ideas from the evening study. Never scheduled from here; each needs Zayn's approval and a figure check at the original source.

## Kenya is courting Gulf travellers

- **Point:** Kenya wants 50,000 visitors a year from the Middle East and got 20,480 last financial year. A lodge whose booking page only speaks English is missing that guest.
- **Source:** Government Delivery Unit figures, reported by Travel Daily News (https://www.traveldailynews.com/statistics-trends/kenya-tourism-growth-arrivals-2026/) and ATTA (https://atta.travel/resource/kenya-tourism-earnings-double-since-2022-as-arrivals-near-2-8-million.html). **Status: not yet checked at the original source.** Find the Government Delivery Unit release before making frames.
- **Frame text:** "20,480 → 50,000. Kenya wants 2.4× more Gulf travellers. Does your lodge's website speak Arabic? Reply 11 for a free bilingual homepage idea."
- **Sells:** 02 website design (bilingual, right-to-left booking page), with 07 social and content as a second service.
- **Look:** split the field: Bone on top carries every word, and a Lake accent field below shows two bars (20,480 and 50,000) at their true ratio, with a source line.

## Mauritius guests stay almost twelve nights

- **Point:** Visitors to Mauritius now stay 11.6 nights on average, and more of them come to work, invest or live for a while, not only for a honeymoon resort. A guest who stays twelve nights needs more than a booking button: laundry, groceries, a desk and someone answering on WhatsApp.
- **Source:** Statistics Mauritius, quoted by Bizcommunity (https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/mauritiuss-evolving-visitor-market-drives-aparthotel-demand-181806a, Oct 2026): 11.6 nights average stay for visitors departing in Q1 2026; record 1.436 million tourists in 2025. Jan–Oct 2026 arrivals 1,191,514, up 4% (Statistics Mauritius via AllAfrica, https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202611130457.html). **Status: not yet checked at the original source.** Open the Statistics Mauritius release before making frames.
- **Frame text:** "11.6 nights. That's how long the average guest now stays in Mauritius. Your website was built for a weekend. Reply 11 for a free long-stay homepage idea."
- **Sells:** 02 website design (long-stay booking page with weekly rates), with 05 AI automation (WhatsApp concierge) as the second service.
- **Look:** the number is the hero in Archivo condensed on Bone; under it a row of twelve small squares, eleven full and one at 60%, Lake accent; one source line. No photo.
