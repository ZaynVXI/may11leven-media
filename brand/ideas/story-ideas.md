## Kenya is courting Gulf travellers

- **Point:** Kenya wants 50,000 visitors a year from the Middle East and got 20,480 last financial year. A lodge whose booking page only speaks English is missing that guest.
- **Source:** Government Delivery Unit figures, reported by Travel Daily News (https://www.traveldailynews.com/statistics-trends/kenya-tourism-growth-arrivals-2026/) and ATTA (https://atta.travel/resource/kenya-tourism-earnings-double-since-2022-as-arrivals-near-2-8-million.html). **Status: not yet checked at the original source.** Find the Government Delivery Unit release before making frames.
- **Frame text:** "20,480 → 50,000. Kenya wants 2.4× more Gulf travellers. Does your lodge's website speak Arabic? Reply 11 for a free bilingual homepage idea."
- **Sells:** 02 website design (bilingual, right-to-left booking page), with 07 social and content as a second service.
- **Look:** split the field: Bone on top carries every word, and a Lake accent field below shows two bars (20,480 and 50,000) at their true ratio, with a source line.
