# Flagship 01: one concept world, all seven services

Status: brief. The idea test runs next; nothing is designed until it's done. Always labelled "concept" when posted.

## Why this instead of single posts
Our single posts were clean but basic: anyone with AI can make one good-looking frame. What a client pays a studio for is a **world with an argument**: a researched problem, one line, and a system that reaches every surface. One deep world posted as a series is worth more than ten one-off concepts (see `brand/learning/minimalism-2026.md`).

## The problem (research, labelled as research)
Small guesthouses in East Africa are found on Instagram and Google, then booked on Booking.com.
- Booking.com commission is usually **15–25%** of the booking (Kolonell, May 2026; a Kenyan host's write-up puts the average near 15%, range 10–30%).
- In Kenya, most small hosts can't take payment upfront through Booking.com, so **no-shows cost them the room and the commission** (muragej, Substack).
- Guests distrust payment links and bank transfers because of scams (same source). That's a trust problem a brand can solve.
- Price parity rules mean the direct offer must win on **perks**, not a lower price (Kolonell).
- Guests discover on Instagram, TikTok and Google, then check the hotel's own site before deciding (Pesapal).
- Award-winning hotel sites keep the booking flow inside their own brand from first page to confirmation and organise menus by what guests want (Profitroom on Grano / Zanzi Resort, Davey Awards 2025).
- Boutique hospitality is moving to "deeply personal": place-rooted materials and local life in the lobby (Hospitality Design).

**Problem sentence:** A nine-room guesthouse in Lamu can't keep the guests it found itself, because the booking happens on a platform that takes up to a quarter of it and leaves no-shows unpaid.

## The concept (fictional client, clearly labelled)
**Kusi House, Lamu Old Town.** Kusi is the southern monsoon that brought dhows to Lamu for centuries. Lamu is a UNESCO site with almost no cars: guests arrive by boat from Manda airport and walk the lanes. That arrival is the experience, and today nobody designs it.

- **Tensions:** heritage vs ease; slow island vs instant booking; personal welcome vs platform anonymity.
- **One line (draft):** "Arrive with the wind." The whole system is about the arrival, from the first Instagram post to the boat at the jetty.
- **Construction idea:** the lateen sail of a dhow, a single triangle with a curved edge. It makes the mark, the pattern, the UI shapes, the key fob and the page transition (the sail fills as the page loads). Same discipline as Kopetzki's stripes and Ivo's curves.
- **Palette:** taken from real materials: coral-stone lime, mangrove-pole brown, indigo dhow-sail shadow, plus one signal colour for "booked". Named, each with a job.
- **Type:** one strict grotesk plus one editorial serif with a reason (quiet premium). Free or licensed only; no Klim test fonts in public work.

## Seven services, one world

| 01 | Brand | Line, voice, mark from the sail, palette, type, guidelines spread | Motto (line → system), Obys/Kopetzki (one motif), Holographik CoMinVi (identity from the client's own material) |

| 05 | Automation | Deposit + reminder flow that cuts no-shows; returning-guest note after 30 days | Axon process rail |
| 06 | AI agent | WhatsApp concierge for "is the boat waiting?" and late arrivals; a person always takes over | FIELD (AI does small useful jobs, people stay in charge), Daisy vs Scammers (one character, one job) |
| 07 | Content | Five-loop launch series + case study carousel | Motto/Ivo five loops, Motto captions, Winterton / WOB / Obrazur reel pacing |

## Presentation plan
1. A phone in a hand on a boat bench at the jetty: the full arrival task.
2. One component on the coral-lime field: the deposit card filling like a sail.
3. The system as a diagram: sail → mark → pattern → UI → key.
4. The key fob in Blender on coral stone, one window light.
5. The live site on a laptop in a lime-washed room, scrolled slowly.
Then the case study carousel and a "View live site" link.

## Gates
1. **Idea test** (founder panel, objection mode): pass if the post answers at least one objection and raises no new confusion.
2. Second opinion from ChatGPT on the brief (asked on the PR).
3. Every piece passes the "could AI make this in one prompt?" test, then preflight Pass 0–3.
