# Concept backlog: real problems, our services

Every idea starts with "X can't do Y because Z". Each one gets the founder idea test (`brand/learning/founder-idea-test.md`) before we design anything, and is always labelled "concept" when posted.

| # | Concept | Problem (X can't Y because Z) | Service | Format |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Check** (scam-check agent) | People in Kenya lose money to "I sent you M-Pesa by mistake" texts because a fake SMS looks exactly like a real one. Forward it to a WhatsApp agent: it compares the message with your real balance and known scam patterns and answers in one line. | 06 AI agents | Phone in a hand on a matatu seat; 3 chat states |
| 2 | **Till** (M-Pesa books) | Small shop owners can't see their weekly profit because their sales sit in M-Pesa statement PDFs. An automation reads the statement, sorts it into sales and stock costs, and sends a Sunday summary on WhatsApp. | 05 AI automation | Statement PDF → summary card; process rail |
| 3 | **Tender** (tender finder) | Small firms miss government tenders because the portals are hard to search and the deadlines are buried. An agent watches the public portals and sends matches with the deadline and a checklist of required documents. | 06 AI agents | Laptop on a stack of files; isometric flow |
| 4 | **Viewing** (honest rentals) | Renters in Nairobi pay viewing fees for flats that don't match the photos. A listing site with dated photos taken on-site, the full monthly cost (rent, service charge, water, deposit) and no viewing fee. | 02 Web | Site on a laptop in an empty flat; scroll recording |
| 5 | **Refill** (water pack) | Gulf households throw away crates of small plastic bottles. A refill-subscription brand with a returnable steel bottle and a delivery app. | 04 Packaging + 03 Apps | Blender bottle hero; app on the counter |
| 6 | **Shop kit** (market brand starter) | Market vendors can't afford a designer, so their stalls all look the same. A free kit: a name check, one mark, a price board, a WhatsApp catalogue cover. It's honest marketing for our brand service. | 01 Brand + 07 Social | Kit flat-lay on a market crate |

Next to build: **#1 Check**. It's the AI agents service, it has a clear harm and it's easy to understand (the same logic as Daisy vs Scammers). Run the idea test first.

## Idea test: Check (10 + 10 simulated buyers)
- v1 (consumer scam checker) and v2 (till payment check for shops): **0 of 10 in each** would comment 11.
- Why: "concept, not a live product" means there is nothing to try. They also want a named business like theirs, a price, and a local contact. v1 also read as not being about their business.
- What would flip them, almost word for word: "a working demo I can test on my own data" and "a clear monthly price I can cancel".
- **Decision:** don't post Check as a mockup. Build it as a real, working free tool (honest red-flag checks, and always "check your balance in the M-Pesa menu"), then post the tool. Waiting on Zayn: hosting it publicly and a published "from" price.
