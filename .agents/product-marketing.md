# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-11-17

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** A minimal creative tech studio that makes good businesses look as good as they are, and work as well as they look.
**What it does:** Seven services under one system: brand identity, websites, apps, packaging and 3D, AI automation, AI agents, and social content. Every project starts from the business problem (lost bookings, low trust, slow sites) and ships something that works, not just something that looks good.
**Product category:** Branding and digital studio (how buyers search: "branding agency", "web design studio", "hotel website design", "brand identity Kenya / Dubai").
**Product type:** Service (project-based), with a planned monthly retainer after launch.
**Business model:** Fixed, named packages with public "from" prices. Approved so far: brand identity from $800. Other prices are Zayn's call. Low-risk first step: "Comment 11" for a free first look (a diagnosis, never free design).

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Growing businesses that sell on looks and trust. Africa first (East Africa tourism, hospitality and real estate in Rwanda, Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, plus every booming industry), then the Gulf, then the rest of the world. First niche: small hotels, guesthouses and boutique stays.
**Decision-makers:** Owners and founders; marketing leads in larger businesses.
**Primary use case:** Their business is better than it looks online, and it costs them customers and margin.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Make us look credible enough that people book or buy direct.
- Give us one system (brand, site, booking, social) that we can run ourselves.
- Take repetitive work off our hands (automation, agents) without losing the human touch.
**Use cases:**
- A guesthouse paying 15–25% commission on bookings it found itself.
- A brand on Instagram only, with no website that proves it's real.
- A product that needs packaging and 3D renders to sell abroad.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Customers decide in seconds, and most of these businesses are better than they look.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Freelancers deliver a logo, not a system, and disappear.
- Big agencies are slow and priced for corporates.
- DIY tools and AI generators look generic and carry the AI label.
- Booking platforms bring guests but take 15–25% and own the relationship.
**What it costs them:** Commission on every stay, lost direct bookings, lower prices, enquiries that never convert.
**Emotional tension:** "We're good at what we do, but online we look like everyone else." Fear of spending on design that doesn't pay back.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Local branding and web agencies — falls short because work is template-led and stops at the logo or the site.
**Secondary:** Freelancers on Fiverr/Upwork, Canva and AI tools — fall short because they look generic and there's no system or strategy.
**Indirect:** Doing nothing and relying on Instagram and booking platforms — falls short because you rent the audience and pay commission forever.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- One studio for brand, web, apps, 3D and AI, so the system holds together.
- Global-studio craft (benchmarked against Motto, Obys, .raw, Lebedev) with local knowledge of African and Gulf markets.
- Problem first: every project names the business problem and how we'll measure it.
- Real tools for real operations: M-Pesa, WhatsApp, cheap phones and slow connections are design inputs, not afterthoughts.
**How we do it differently:** Research, then a system, then real screens and devices, then proof.
**Why that's better:** It looks premium and it works, so it pays back.
**Why customers choose us:** To be confirmed with real clients. Hypothesis: craft at a global level, priced for growing businesses, built for their market.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "These are concepts. Where are real clients and results?" (top objection in every simulated panel) | Lead with the free first look and pilot projects; publish honest results with permission; until then, show working tools and original research, clearly labelled. |
| "Guests trust Booking.com more than my site." | Keep the platforms for discovery; win the repeat and referred guests direct with a site that feels as safe as a platform (deposit, clear policy, real photos, WhatsApp). |
| "What does $800 actually buy?" | Name the package and list the deliverables; show a before and after; offer the diagnosis first. |

**Anti-persona:** Businesses shopping on price alone, anyone wanting AI-generated logos overnight, and clients who want spec work before any agreement.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Paying commission; looking amateur next to competitors; enquiries that don't convert.
**Pull:** A premium look, direct bookings, a system they can run themselves.
**Habit:** "Instagram is enough"; the nephew who does the website; existing booking engines.
**Anxiety:** Spending on design that doesn't pay back; trusting a new studio without references; losing platform visibility.

## Customer Language
**How they describe the problem:** (simulated panels, to confirm with real owners)
- "I'm not going to pay $800 to a studio to move my guests off Booking.com."
- "I need to see something working before I hand over my business."
- "I already pay for a booking engine and a channel manager."
**How they describe us:** No real customer quotes yet.
**Words to use:** book direct, look as good as you are, one system, works on any phone, free first look.
**Words to avoid:** cutting-edge, innovative, synergy, "AI-powered" as a selling point, anything that hints the page is run by AI.
**Glossary:**
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|

## Brand Voice
| Attribute | Means | Doesn't mean | Example line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quiet | Few words, one idea per line | Cold or vague | "Most businesses are better than they look." |
| Precise | Numbers, places, real details | Jargon | "You arrive by boat." |
| Confident | Says what we do and why | Boasting | "Websites that load, show a price and take the booking." |
| Warm | Speaks to the owner as a person | Casual slang | "We'll look at yours, free." |

**Tone by context:**
- Support: plain, calm, helpful; point to hello@may11levenlabs.com.
- Launch: one strong line, one image, one ask.
- Errors: own it plainly, fix it, no drama.
- Sales: diagnosis first, never a pitch.
- Social: short lines, real work, Comment 11. Never pidgin; UK or US slang only when needed.
**Terms we own:** Comment 11, free first look, Second Thoughts (series), Field Study (series).
**Never use (and why):** Zayn's name (we sign as the company); any hint the page is run by AI; fake testimonials or invented results (trust is our weakest point).
