what if we rebranded bathu?

bathu is township slang for shoes. it started in a small room in alex and now walks the whole country.

so the mark is a step: two soles, one ahead of the other. one in ink, one in gum rubber, the sole every sneaker kid knows. the colours come from the street, not a palette: concrete, the kerb, a white midsole.

unsolicited concept. not affiliated with bathu.
