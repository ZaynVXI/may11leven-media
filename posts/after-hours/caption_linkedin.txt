21:47. A guest asks a small hotel in Lamu whether the roof suite is free in February.

Reception closed at 21:00. On most hotel websites, that question waits until morning, and by morning the guest has often booked somewhere else.

After hours is a concept we designed for Kusi House, our concept hotel: an assistant that answers in the hotel's own voice, quotes the real price, takes a 20% deposit by M-Pesa or card, and hands reception one summary at 07:00.

Inside:
→ The question at 21:47, and the answer
→ The booking card: $880 for four nights, $176 to hold it
→ The questions only a good host answers: a cot for a baby, a late flight
→ The 07:00 summary, with the one enquiry it leaves for a person

It is a concept, and the numbers are illustrative. The rule behind it is real: an assistant should book what is simple and hand over what needs a person.
