## References and steal list
- Motto workbook covers: a hairline grid on a dark field with one big numeral. We take the grid as structure, in Infrared on Ink.
- Obys, Kopetzki "How we help": numbered service cards, one in the accent colour. We take the numbered index for the seven services.
- .raw: one idea per page, lots of air. We take the restraint.

## Judgement brief
1. Idea: most businesses are better than they look; we close the gap.
2. Hero: the two-bar 11 mark, measured on a grid.
3. Surface: Ink field with Infrared hairlines (cover, close); Bone paper inside.
4. Light: flat, printed-document light; the work pages carry their own render light.
5. Framing: 1080 x 1350, 72 px margins, 12-column grid.
6. Human trace: the work pages (real renders of a product and a phone in use).
7. Slot: LinkedIn document, 8 pages. Borrowed move: Swiss grid.
