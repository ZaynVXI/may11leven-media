Can a product look ready to sell before the first unit is made?

That's the question behind ata, a pepper oil from Lagos that doesn't exist. We gave it a name, a label and a bottle, built and lit it in 3D three different ways, and cut a short launch film, all before a single bottle was filled.

For a founder, that means three things. You can test packaging with real customers before paying for a print run. Your launch visuals are ready on launch day. And retailers or investors see a finished product instead of a sketch.

Every image in this document is a 3D render. Nothing was photographed.
