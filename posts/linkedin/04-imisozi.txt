For many businesses in East Africa, the phone is the shop. Customers find you on Instagram, ask on WhatsApp and pay by mobile money, often without ever opening a laptop.

So when we designed Imisozi, a concept coffee roaster in Kigali, we started with the phone, not the desktop:

- Three single-origin coffees, each with packaging in the colours of the hills it comes from
- Choose an origin and a grind in two taps
- Pay with MoMo or card
- Delivery in Kigali within 48 hours

The desktop site came last, built from the same system.
