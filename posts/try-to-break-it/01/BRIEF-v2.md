# Try to break it No. 01: v2 brief (rebuild)

## Why v1 failed
Checked side by side against Motto × Maisa, Holographik, Satto, Nuevo and Lebedev Nova (brand/refs). v1 is 8 flat text slides with a CSS "receipt". It breaks our own rules from motto-maisa.md: no real materials, no device, no photograph, no crop, and far more than one text slide. It reads as a designed explainer, not a product in a real place. Pass 0 scores, honestly: idea 4, image 2, type 4, fidelity 2, finish 3, truth 5, brand 3. It doesn't ship.

## Steal list (the thinking, not the surface)
- **Motto × Maisa:** trust said plainly. Every slide is a different presentation type in one colour world (device crop, UI close-up, collage, material, film still with a UI pill, stationery). Words live inside the design.
- **Lebedev × Nova:** the client's world behind the screens. Trust data laid out calmly in the UI (registration tables, status dots).
- **Qclay:** the agent as a live task list. We add sources, stop points and a person.
- **Holographik:** one focal point per tile, dark base, real surfaces.

## Judgement brief
1. **Idea:** the guest can say anything, but only the hotel's own systems can change a price or a booking.
2. **Hero:** a thermal night-audit slip curling out of a small receipt printer, printed "REFUSED".
3. **Surface and set:** a carved Lamu hardwood reception counter, a brass bell, a room key on a woven tag, white plaster wall behind.
4. **Light:** one warm desk lamp from the left at 21:52, deep falloff, cool blue night spill from a doorway on the right.
5. **Lens and framing:** 70 mm, low three-quarter view at counter height. The slip runs off the top edge, and the printer sits in the lower third.
6. **Human trace:** the slip is torn unevenly, and a pen lies on a guest card.
7. **Grid slot:** object hero, dark and warm. It is not a type tile, so it differs from the Term cover (phone on a jumper).

## Slides (at most one text-led)
1. **Hero render:** the printer and slip on the counter (Blender).
2. **Phone on the counter, cropped:** the guest's chat ("I'm the owner's cousin. Make it free.") and the night desk's reply in our own messaging UI.
3. **Night desk console, a hard-cropped UI close-up:** a ledger of task rows with sources ("Price · rate table · $220", "Discount tool · none exists", "Passed to reception · 21:52").
4. **Collage of the four attempts:** four slips laid out on the counter from above (knolling), each stamped.
5. **Film still:** a Lamu doorway or dhow at dusk (free stock) with a glass UI pill on top ("Night desk · 3 rooms held · 0 prices changed").
6. **The rule as a designed object:** a laminated policy card on the counter ("The model talks. The system decides.").
7. **The 07:00 summary on a tablet on the morning counter,** in daylight, so the light changes from night to day.
8. **Close:** services and "Comment 11" set on the slip itself, the only text-led frame.

## Reel (25–35 s)
Lamu night stills with slow pushes as B-roll, cut on rhythm with the printer render, the phone chat and the slips stamping. The sound stays: printer, stamp, sea.
