9.6 seconds.

That's how long the median independent hotel website in Zanzibar takes to show its main image on a mid-range phone. We tested all 51.

This is our first post here, so it starts the way we work: with research, not opinions.

Field Study 01. OpenStreetMap lists 107 hotels, guest houses and hostels on Unguja with their own website. After removing chains, dead links and duplicates, 51 independent places had a site that works. We opened each homepage the way a guest would.

What we found:
→ 86% are slower than Google's "poor" line of 4 seconds.
→ About 1 in 6 listed hotel websites is gone or has been taken over.
→ Half send the "Book" button to a different web address.
→ 16% show a room price.
→ 2 of 51 give guests a reason to book direct.

Why it matters: guests find a small hotel on Instagram or through a friend, then check its website on their phone. When the page is slow, shows no price and sends them elsewhere, the booking often goes to a booking site, with commission paid on a guest the hotel found itself.

The fixes aren't expensive. Compress the main image. Show a "from" price. Keep booking on your own address. Give one reason to book direct. Add WhatsApp and local payment.

Method: Google Lighthouse 12, mobile profile, three runs per site, median used. Lab estimates, not data from real guests. Homepages only. Prices and offers checked by eye. No business is named, and per-site results stay private.
