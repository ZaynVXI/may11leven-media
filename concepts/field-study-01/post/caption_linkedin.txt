We opened every independent hotel website in Zanzibar on a mid-range phone. Here is what a guest sees.

Field Study 01. OpenStreetMap lists 107 hotels, guest houses and hostels on Unguja with their own website. After removing chains, dead links and duplicates, 51 independent places had a website that works.

What we found:
→ The median homepage takes 9.6 seconds to show its main image or headline.
→ 86% are slower than Google's "poor" line of 4 seconds.
→ About 1 in 6 listed hotel websites is gone or has been taken over.
→ Half send the "Book" button to a different web address.
→ 16% show a room price. Only 2 of 51 give a reason to book direct.

Why it matters: most guests find a small hotel on Instagram or through a friend, then check the website on a phone before they book. If that page is slow, shows no price and sends them somewhere else, the booking often ends up on a booking site, with commission on a guest the hotel found itself.

Method: Google Lighthouse 12, mobile profile, three runs per site, median used. These are lab estimates, not data from real guests. Homepages only. Prices and offers were checked by eye. No business is named, and per-site results stay private.
