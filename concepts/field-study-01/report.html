06What to fix firstField study 01

Five fixes,
in the order we'd do them.

01Compress the hero image. On 7 in 10 sites it's what the guest is waiting for.
02Show a "from" price on the homepage.
03Keep booking on your own address, in your own colours.
04Give one reason to book direct: the transfer, breakfast, a late checkout.
05Add WhatsApp and the payment methods your guests already use.

And check the link on your map listing still opens your website.

None of these needs a new website07 / 08
