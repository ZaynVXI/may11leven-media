# Kusi House site v2: the plan

Bar: Obys (Kopetzki) and .raw. Brief: brand/ideas/flagship-01.md. Notes: brand/learning/obys.md, raw-site.md.

## One motif: the lateen sail
- The mark's curved edge becomes the system: image masks with one curved edge, section ends that sweep like a sail, a progress bar that fills like a sail, numbered cards with a sail notch.
- Never stripes, never stone objects (those belong to Obys and .raw).

## Pages (9)
1. Home: hero, the four facts as big numbers, three ways to stay, the arrival strip, rooms, the house, close.
2. Rooms: three rooms, each with photos, light by hour, price from.
3. Arriving: flight to door timeline, boat, tide, lane map.
4. Book: dates, room, perks, 20% deposit by M-Pesa or card, confirmation, all on-brand.
5. The house: story, coral stone, mangrove poles, the roof.
6. Lamu: no cars, the lanes, the waterfront, what to do.
7. Questions: plain answers (deposit, boat, children, prayer times).
8. Confirmation: "See you at the jetty."
9. 404: "Lost in the lanes."

## Build order
Home first (this commit), then Book and Arriving (the product), then the rest. Then devices (Blender), recordings and the page-wall reel.
