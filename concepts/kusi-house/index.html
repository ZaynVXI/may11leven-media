For centuries the kusi, the southern monsoon, carried dhows into Lamu. We named the house after it, and built the stay around one thing most hotels forget: the arrival.

The house is eighteenth-century coral rag and lime plaster, restored with the masons who still work in the old town.

Every room opens to the roof. The sea is four lanes away.
