Kusi House, part 2: the website. A concept.

Most hotel sites send you somewhere else the moment you press "Book". This one doesn't.

The booking stays on the house's own page, in its own colours, to the last step. A small deposit holds the room by M-Pesa or card. The day before, the guest gets the tide and the boat time. The direct perk is the boat from Manda and breakfast on the roof, not a lower price.

Part 1 was the brand. Next: the arrival page.
