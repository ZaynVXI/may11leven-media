Kusi House, part 1: the brand. A concept.

A nine-room guesthouse in Lamu Old Town, where there are no cars and you arrive by boat.

The problem is real. Guests find small houses like this on Instagram, then book on a platform that keeps 15 to 25% of the stay.

So the brand starts with the arrival. Kusi is the monsoon that brought dhows to Lamu. The mark is one lateen sail. The colours come from the island: coral-stone walls, mangrove roof poles, sail canvas, the channel at dusk.
