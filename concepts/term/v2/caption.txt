concept: term. a school fees app for parents in kenya that saves a little every friday, so january doesn't arrive as one big bill.

each child gets a card in their school's colours. every friday, a small amount moves from m-pesa into a locked pot that can only pay the school. when term opens, it pays the paybill and keeps the bursar's receipt.

one calm status per child: covered, or on track with the fridays left. swipe to watch a friday land.
