concept: term. a school fees app that saves a little every friday, so january doesn't arrive as one big bill.

fees come three times a year. pay comes every week or month. term splits each child's next term into small weekly amounts from m-pesa, locks them until the school is paid, and keeps the receipt.

one calm status per child: covered, or on track with the amount still to go.
