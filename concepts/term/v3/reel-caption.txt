january arrives as one bill. term spreads it over the fridays before it.

concept: term, a school fees app for parents in kenya. every friday a little moves from m-pesa into a locked pot that can only pay the school. each saving is a tick in the register. when term opens, it pays and keeps the receipt.

and when something goes wrong: it only pays checked school paybills, unlocks in an emergency after 48 hours with both parents, asks before raising the friday amount, and tells you if m-pesa fails.
