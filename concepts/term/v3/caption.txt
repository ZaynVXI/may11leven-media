concept: term. a school fees app for parents in kenya. every friday it saves a little from m-pesa, so january doesn't arrive as one big bill.

each child has a name tape in their school's colours and a register of fridays. every saving lands as a red tick. when term opens, it pays the school and keeps the receipt.

the part most money apps skip: what happens when something goes wrong. term only pays school paybills we've checked against the school's bank letter. in an emergency, the money unlocks after 48 hours with both parents agreeing. if fees go up, it works out the new friday amount and asks first. if m-pesa fails, it tries again on saturday and tells you.
